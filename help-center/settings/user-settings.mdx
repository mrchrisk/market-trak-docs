User settings control your personal profile and preferences. These are specific to your account, not your workspace, so team members each have their own settings.

## Accessing user settings

Click your **profile icon** in the sidebar, then select **User settings**. Or navigate directly to `/user-settings`.

## Profile

Update your personal information:

- **Contact name** - your display name shown in the sidebar and to team members

- **Email address** - your login email (changing this requires verification)

- **Notification email** - optionally set a different email for notifications (if you want alerts sent somewhere other than your login email). New leads can go somewhere different again, see [Where notifications are sent](#where-notifications-are-sent) below

## Notification preferences

Control which notifications you receive and how:

| Event                  | Email                                           | Push                      |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| **Report ready**       | When a report finishes generating               | Browser push notification |
| **Content Pack ready**   | When your Content Pack finishes generating        | Browser push notification |
| **New lead**           | When a new lead is captured                     | Browser push notification |
| **Campaign sent**      | When an email campaign completes                | Browser push notification |
| **Posts ready**        | When property feed posts are ready for approval | Browser push notification |
| **Credits low**        | When your credit balance is running low         | Browser push notification |
| **Post published**     | When a scheduled post is published              | Browser push notification |

Toggle each notification on or off per channel. You can enable email notifications, push notifications, or both. Turning off **Content Pack ready** email stops the "Your Content Pack is ready" delivery email landing in your inbox when a pack finishes generating; turning off the push toggle stops the matching browser push notification. The two channels are independent, so you can keep one and silence the other.

### Where notifications are sent

Two boxes sit below the toggles, and they decide the destination rather than whether an email is sent at all.

- **Send notifications to** - every notification email goes here. Leave it empty and they go to your account email.

- **Send new lead notifications to** - new-lead emails only. Use it when enquiries are handled on a shared address such as `enquiries@` or `valuations@`, and you still want report, campaign and credit alerts in your own mailbox. Leave it empty and lead emails go to the address above, exactly as before.

The greyed-out text in each box shows the address it would use right now, so you can always see where leads are going. Both settings are yours alone: a colleague on the same workspace can point their lead emails somewhere else, and if two of you choose the same address it still arrives once.

To stop lead emails altogether rather than redirect them, turn off the **New lead** email toggle in the table above.

### Push notifications

To receive browser push notifications:

1. Click **Enable Push Notifications** in your notification settings

2. Your browser will ask for permission to show notifications

3. Once enabled, you will receive real-time alerts even when the Market Trak tab is not active

Push notifications work in Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari.

## AI Assistant settings

- **Custom instructions** - write permanent instructions for your AI assistant (see [Memory and Instructions](/help-center/ai-assistant/memory-and-instructions))

- **Memory toggle** - enable or disable the auto-learning memory feature
