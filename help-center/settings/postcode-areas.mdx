Your plan covers a set number of postcode districts, and those are your areas. A district is the first half of a postcode, so CM15 covers every CM15 postcode and every street in it.

Almost every agent works a handful of districts, and your areas are simply those districts written down. They decide where your reports and Content Packs are made, which areas the assistant suggests, and which leads your campaigns treat as local.

## Where to find them

Go to **Workspace Settings**, open the **Billing** tab, and look for **Your postcode areas**, just under your credits.

The card shows every area you cover, how many you are using, and how many you have left.

## How an area is added

There are two ways, and neither of them can happen by accident.

<Steps>
<Step title="By making something for it" icon="file-text" titleType="p">
The first time you generate a report or a Content Pack for a district, it becomes one of your areas. If it is a new district and you have room, you are asked to confirm first, so you always know when one is about to be used.
</Step>
<Step title="By adding it yourself" icon="plus" titleType="p">
On the **Your postcode areas** card, type the district into the box and press **Add**. The button then asks you to confirm, and pressing it again adds the area.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
Your first area is added quietly, without asking. Nobody's first area is a mistake, and there is nothing to weigh up until you have one.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="alert">
Areas stay with your account once added. They cannot be swapped for a different district later, so add the ones you actually work.
</Callout>

## What your areas change

- **Reports and Content Packs** are made for areas you cover. Ask for one somewhere new and you are asked to confirm, or told when your areas are all in use.

- **The assistant** offers your areas when it asks where something is for, and pulls live market data for them. Ask it about a district you do not cover and it offers to add it, rather than simply saying no. It shows your four most recent areas as buttons, and you can always type any other one.

- **Campaigns** suggest your own areas first. Leads outside them are never hidden: they are marked **Out of area** on their record, and there is a separate **Out of area** audience so you can email them whenever you want to.

- **Instant valuations** are not affected at all. Your valuation form works for any visitor, whatever their postcode, and every lead is captured and kept.

## Buying more areas

When every area is in use, the **Your postcode areas** card offers extra ones.

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose how many" icon="plus" titleType="p">
Extra areas are £25 a month each. Use the plus and minus buttons to pick how many you want.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the amount" icon="receipt" titleType="p">
You are shown exactly what you will be charged today before anything is taken. Because the areas are added part way through your billing month, that first charge is usually less than the full price.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm" icon="check" titleType="p">
The areas are added to your existing plan straight away. There is no second subscription and no second renewal date: everything stays on the bill you already have.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
If you find yourself buying several areas, check whether the next plan up is better value. It usually includes more areas along with everything else.
</Callout>

## Areas that switch off

If you ever hold more areas than your plan covers, the newest ones switch off rather than being deleted. They appear in red on the card, marked as switched off.

A switched-off area behaves as though you never had it: no new reports or Content Packs, no market data from the assistant, and its leads count as out of area. Nothing you already made is touched, and everything you published stays exactly where it is.

They are not gone. Add the room again, with an extra area or a bigger plan, and the longest-held one comes straight back as it was. The card offers a button to switch a specific area back on. There is also a small cross beside each switched-off area if you would rather clear it out for good.

## Common questions

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Can I swap one area for another?" default-open="false">
Not yourself. Areas are permanent once added, which is what keeps them meaningful. If you have genuinely moved your patch, get in touch and we will sort it out.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What happens to reports for an area I no longer cover?" default-open="false">
They stay readable and shareable exactly as they are, and any link you have shared keeps working. What you cannot do is regenerate them with fresh data until the area is covered again.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Do instant valuations use up an area?" default-open="false">
No. Your valuation form works for any postcode, and every lead it captures is stored and counted as normal. Only creating content uses an area.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Does a Property Pack use an area?" default-open="false">
No. A Property Pack is about one property you are already selling, so it works wherever that property is.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="I only asked the assistant a question. Why did it mention my areas?" default-open="false">
Live market data for a district is the same information a report is built from, so it comes out of your areas too. If you have room, the assistant offers to add the district before it looks anything up, and nothing is added until you say yes.
</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
