The Approval Queue is where automatically generated posts (from the [Property Feed](/help-center/social-media/property-feed)) land before going live. It gives you full control over what gets published on your social media accounts.

## Accessing the queue

1. Click **Content Calendar** in the sidebar

2. Click the **Approval Queue** link near the top of the calendar (or go to `/social-media/approval-queue`)

## Reviewing posts

Each post in the queue shows:

- The **scheduled date and time** at the top, in **green** while it is still upcoming and **red** once it has passed

- The **post style** (Conversational, Insight-led, or Short & Punchy)

- The **property photo**, taken from the listing itself. Each post about a property gets a different photo where the listing has more than one

- The **caption** text

- The **source feed** it came from

## Actions

### Approve

Click **Approve** to schedule the post for the date and time shown at the top. You choose which platforms (Facebook, Instagram) to schedule it to, and it is added to your content calendar to publish automatically at that time. This is the main action on the card, so it is the pink button on the right.

### Publish Now

Click **Publish Now** to post it to your connected accounts straight away, without waiting for the scheduled time.

### Edit the caption and date

Click the **pencil** at the top of the caption to edit the post. You can change the caption, replace or AI-edit the image, and adjust the scheduled date and time together, then click **Save**.

### Change just the date

If you only want to reschedule, click the **date** at the top of the card. Pick a new date and time and click **Set date**. The rest of the post stays as it is. This is the same date picker you use on Content Pack posts, so it behaves identically in both places, including on a phone.

### Remove

Click the **thumbs-down** at the bottom-left of the card to remove the post from the queue. It will not be published. If you change your mind, click **Undo** on the message that appears; once that message goes, the post does not come back, because the property it was written for has already been through your feed.

Removing several at once works the same way. Tick the posts you want gone, click **Remove Selected**, and confirm. That confirmation is worth reading: the credits spent generating those posts are not refunded, and any captions you rewrote or images you replaced go with them.

## Posts with a date in the past

If a post's scheduled date has already passed, it is flagged in **red** at the top of the card, but you can still click **Approve** as normal. The approve dialog tells you the date has passed and the button becomes **Update date and approve**: the post gets a fresh date starting tomorrow (in a bulk approval, each past-dated post gets its own fresh day, one a day, while posts with future dates keep them). Prefer a specific date? Click the date on the card to pick one. And **Publish Now** always posts it immediately.

## Auto-approve

If you prefer hands-off automation, you can enable **auto-approve** for specific platforms in your Property Feed settings. When auto-approve is on, posts skip the queue entirely and publish (or schedule) automatically.

> **Caution:** Auto-approve means posts go live without your review. Make sure you are happy with the AI output quality before enabling this.

## Queue management tips

- Check the queue at least once a day when you have an active Property Feed

- Posts are most effective when published within a few days of the listing going live

- If your queue is building up, consider enabling auto-approve for one platform to reduce the backlog

- Edit captions to add your personal touch, a brief local insight makes a big difference
