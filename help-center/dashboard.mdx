The dashboard is your command centre. It gives you an instant overview of your marketing activity, lead generation, and content performance across your workspace.

<Image src="https://db.marketrak.pro/storage/v1/object/public/broadcast-assets/docs/docs-dashboard.png" width="1920" height="1080" alt="The dashboard with the Awareness meter, Today's Focus card, metric cards and recent activity" />

## Awareness Meter

The large featured tile at the top of your dashboard is the **Awareness Meter**. It scores your overall marketing activity on a scale from 0 to 100 based on:

- How many reports you have generated recently

- Email campaigns you have sent

- Social media posts you have scheduled or published

- Leads you have captured

The score decays over time if you are not active, encouraging consistent marketing habits. Think of it as your "marketing fitness" tracker.

<Callout kind="info">
Posts only count toward your Awareness score once they are scheduled on the calendar or published. Saving a post to your Library is a useful first step, but a saved post sitting on the shelf doesn't yet grow your audience. If you see a low Posts number on your dashboard, that is the meter calling out the gap between what you have written and what you have actually put out there.
</Callout>

## Metric cards

Below the Awareness Meter you will see quick-glance metric cards:

| Metric           | What it tracks                              | Colour       |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| **Leads**        | New leads captured this period              | Amber/Orange |
| **Campaigns**    | Email campaigns sent                        | Green        |
| **Social Posts** | Posts scheduled on the calendar or published | Purple       |

Each card shows the current count plus a trend compared to the previous period. Hover over a card for additional detail. The Social Posts card subtitle reads "Scheduled & posted" to make the definition obvious at a glance.

## Today's Focus

The **Today's Focus** card suggests your next best action based on your recent activity, one focus at a time. For example:

- When a new listing has just arrived from your property feed or CRM, the card becomes **Create a Property Pack for [address]** with the listing's photo. One click opens the Property Pack builder with that property already selected, so you can turn it into posts while the listing is still news. This suggestion appears on paid plans and during the free trial.

- Posts waiting in your Approval Queue, with a link to review them

- Content that is over a month old, with a suggestion to create a fresh Content Pack for that postcode

- "You haven't posted to social media this week" with a link to create a post

- Leads waiting for a follow-up email, with a link to your leads page

- If everything is up to date, you will see an "All Caught Up" message

## Engagement Snapshot

Between the metric cards and recent activity, a compact **Engagement Snapshot** widget shows your last 7 days of engagement:

- **Report views** - how many times your public reports were viewed
- **Email opens** - how many campaign emails were opened by leads
- **Top report** - the title of your best-performing report

Clicking the report views figure takes you to the Reports Engagement tab. Clicking email opens takes you to the Leads Email Engagement tab.

The widget hides automatically when there is no engagement data to show.

## Recent Activity

A timeline of your latest actions across the workspace:

- Reports generated

- Email campaigns sent

- Social media posts published

- Leads captured

Click any item to go directly to the relevant page.

## Refreshing data

Dashboard metrics update automatically and cache for 2 minutes to keep things fast. If you have just completed an action (like sending a campaign) and the numbers have not updated yet, wait a moment or click the refresh button.

Changes you make in one browser tab will sync to other open tabs automatically.
