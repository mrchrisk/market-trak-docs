The **Emails** page is where you write emails to your leads, send them now or later, set up emails that repeat every month, and see every email you have sent. Find it in the sidebar just under **Leads**, in the **Nurture** group, or under **People** on your phone.

The page has two tabs:

- **Sent & scheduled** shows, from the top, your **Repeating** emails (when you have any), then **Scheduled** (what is waiting to go), then **Sent** (every email sent in the last 12 months)
- **Drafts & templates** lists the emails you have started and the templates you can start from, with buttons to view, edit, duplicate and delete them

The cog at the top right opens your email settings.

Emails to your leads are part of every paid plan and your free trial. The Free plan can't send them: pressing **New email** there offers you a trial or a plan instead, and a send is turned down with "Your plan doesn't include emails to leads. Choose a plan, or start a trial, to send them."

## Sending an email

Press **Send Campaign** on the **Leads** page, **Send email** on a lead's record, or the email button on a report. **Choose an email** opens, with your drafts and templates in one list.

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose the email" icon="mouse-pointer-click" titleType="p">
Press the eye on a row to see the email large first, if you like. Then press **Choose this template**, or **Choose this draft** to send one you have started.
</Step>
<Step title="Check it and send" icon="send" titleType="p">
The email opens as your leads will see it, with the **Send** panel beside it: the report, who it goes to, when, and **Send**. To change the words, press **Edit** above the email, then **Preview** to see it again.
</Step>
</Steps>

If you ticked leads first, or started from a report, the line at the top of **Choose an email** says so, and the email goes to just those people, with that report. A draft you choose takes them too, in place of its own. **Cancel** takes you back where you were, and **Choose an email** at the top of the email brings you back to the list to pick another. Nothing is sent until you press **Send**.

## Writing an email

Press **New email**, or click a template on **Drafts & templates** to start a new email from it. A blank email opens ready to write; one from a template opens on its preview, so press **Edit** to change it. On a phone there is no **New email** button: start from a template or a draft on **Drafts & templates**.

<Steps>
<Step title="Write the subject and preview line" icon="type" titleType="p">
The **preview line** is the short grey text inboxes show after the subject. It is optional. Use the **+** in the subject box to add a merge field, such as the lead's first name.
</Step>
<Step title="Write the email" icon="pencil" titleType="p">
Type as you would in any editor. The toolbar has bold, italic, links, bullet points and headings, and the blocks you can add: **Image**, **Video**, **Button**, **Report card**, **Newest homes** and **Divider**. Press **Insert**, or type **/**, to add a merge field. To add your logo, press **Image** and pick one of your saved logos: it goes in small, like a letterhead.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the preview" icon="eye" titleType="p">
Press **Preview** above the email to see it as your leads will, with the merge fields and report figures filled in, and **Edit** to go back to writing. Switch between **Desktop** and **Phone** to check both. If the email shows a report's figures, the preview asks you to choose the report first, so you never mistake sample figures for what goes out.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose who it goes to and when" icon="send" titleType="p">
The **Send** panel beside the email (under it on a phone) sets the report when the email shows one, who gets it, and when it goes. See below.
</Step>
</Steps>

Your email saves itself as a draft as you type. If you leave an email you started, you are asked whether to keep it as a draft.

Every email ends with a footer showing your agency name and a working unsubscribe link. You can change the footer's wording, but the unsubscribe link can't be removed. To add your office address under your agency name in new emails, fill in **Address in the footer (optional)** in your email settings.

### Adding a button

Press **Button** on the toolbar, type the button's words, and choose what it **Opens**:

- **A web address** you type in
- **Your website**, the Business Website in **Settings > Branding**
- **Your booking link**, the Calendar Booking Link in **Settings > Branding**
- **Your instant valuation page**, if your plan includes it
- **Your property search page**, taken from your [property feed](/help-center/social-media/property-feed) when it is a page on your website

Choose one of your own pages and the button starts with suitable words, such as "See all our homes", which you can change. The box shows the page it will open. If you haven't set that page up, it says so, and the button is left out of your emails until you do.

### Adding a report card

A report card puts a report's headline figures in the email, with a **Read the full report** button.

1. Press **Report card** on the toolbar
2. Choose the report
3. Tick up to three figures, such as the average asking price and the price change
4. Press **Add report card**

Only shared reports are listed, because a lead cannot open a report that is not shared. You do not need a report to send an email.

If the report has no reliable figure for **Days on market**, the card shows that box as **Left out**, and the email goes without it.

### Adding your newest homes

The **Newest homes** block shows three of your newest homes from your property feed, each with its photo, price, bedrooms, street and a **View this home** link to its listing.

1. Press **Newest homes** (the house) on the toolbar
2. Change the heading if you like. It starts as "Our newest homes"
3. Choose a **Style**: **List** puts each photo beside its details; **Grid** puts the homes side by side, and one under another on a phone
4. Choose what to **Show**: homes **For sale**, **To let**, or **Both**
5. Press **Add newest homes**

To change any of these later, click the block and press **Edit**.

**When the homes are chosen.** A repeating email puts in your newest homes each time it goes, so every month shows that month's. An email you send once shows the homes it holds when you press **Send** or **Schedule**, and a scheduled email sends exactly those. Homes marked sold, under offer or let agreed are left out. If there are no homes of the kind you chose, the block is left out, heading and all.

**Choosing the homes in a one-off email.** In an email you send once, the block's **Edit** box lists your nine newest homes with the first three ticked. Untick any you don't want, or tick a different one, up to three. To find a particular home, such as one you mentioned on the phone, search by street, postcode or price: the homes you have ticked stay at the top. If a home you ticked has gone from your feed by the time you send, it's left out. The ticks are only for that email: if you set it to repeat, or save it as a template, it shows your newest homes instead.

The block is on the toolbar once your [property feed](/help-center/social-media/property-feed) has homes in it.

<Callout kind="tip">
Images you add are shrunk for email automatically, so they load quickly in your leads' inboxes. A video shows as its picture with a play button, which opens the video.
</Callout>

## Who it goes to

The Send panel gives you these choices:

- **All your areas** sends to everyone in your areas, with the number of people shown. On a plan that doesn't set your postcode areas, this reads **Everyone** and includes every lead
- **Choose areas** lets you tick postcode districts, each shown with its town, how many people it reaches, and when its leads were last emailed, for example "Emailed 2 days ago" or "Never emailed"
- **Out of area** sends to leads outside your areas, including those with no area. It only appears when your plan sets your [postcode areas](/help-center/settings/postcode-areas) and some of your leads are outside them

A lead is in a district if any of the **areas of interest** on their record matches it, so one lead can be reached for every area they have asked about. Each lead's areas build up from their enquiries, and you can edit them on the lead's record. A lead whose location is only a town name, with no postcode, has no area, so emails to your areas don't reach them until you give them one. See [Managing Leads](/help-center/leads/managing-leads#the-lead-record).

To email particular people, tick them on the **Leads** page (up to 5,000 at a time) and press **Send Campaign**. **Choose an email** opens with them chosen: choose a template or a draft, or start from a blank email, and the email goes to exactly the people you ticked. If you keep it as a draft, it still goes to just those people when you open it again from **Drafts**.

When your email has a report and your plan sets your postcode areas, **Who it goes to** starts on that report's area (if you have leads there), so each area's leads get their own area's figures. If you choose other areas as well, the panel tells you, for example "Leads in SG12 will get CM15's figures", and you can still send. To email several areas each with its own figures, set up one email (or one repeating email) per area, each with that area's report.

The panel shows how many people it goes to and how many credits it uses before you send. Unsubscribed and bounced leads are always left out.

## When it goes

- **Now** sends it straight away
- **Later** sends it at the date and time you pick
- **Repeat** sends it every month or every week (see [Repeating emails](#repeating-emails))

Press **Send a test to me** to see the email in your own inbox first. When you send or schedule, you are asked to confirm, with the number of people and the credits it uses.

When an email has finished sending, we email you to say how many people it reached. That email, and the ones we send when an email pauses or a repeating email skips a send, only come while **Campaign completed** is switched on under **Notifications** in your **Profile & preferences** (see [User Settings](/help-center/settings/user-settings#notification-preferences)). It is on unless you have turned it off.

### If its report is from an earlier month

When your email shows figures from a report made before this month, the confirm box says so. If your credits cover both updating the report and sending, it offers **Update and send** (or **Update and schedule**): the report is regenerated with this month's figures, just as pressing **Regenerate report** on the report's own page would, and then your email goes. Updating uses the usual report credits, and the box says how many. Choose **Send as it is** (or **Schedule as it is**) to keep the figures you have.

If your credits don't cover both, the box says how many more credits updating it as well would need, and **Send** (or **Schedule**) sends it with the figures it has.

If the report can't be updated, nothing is sent and your email stays in your drafts.

### Who a scheduled email reaches

When you schedule an email to your areas, you choose who it reaches when the time comes:

- **Everyone in** those areas **at the time** (the default) sends to the people on the list now, plus anyone who joins those areas before it goes out. This is usually what you want, since more leads arrive while you wait.
- **Only the people on the list now** fixes the list when you schedule. Anyone who joins afterwards does not get it.

This choice only appears for emails sent to your areas. If you chose particular people, the list is always fixed. Anyone who unsubscribes before it goes out is removed automatically.

### Changing or cancelling

When you schedule, the confirm box reminds you: "Until it goes, you can change its time on the Emails page, or cancel it and it goes back to your drafts."

Scheduled emails wait under **Scheduled** on **Sent & scheduled**, below your repeating emails, showing when each one goes and how many people it reaches. On its row, press **Change time** to move it, or **Cancel** to stop it, right up until it sends. A cancelled email goes back to your drafts, so you can change it and send it again.

## Repeating emails

A repeating email works out who it goes to each time it sends, so new leads in its areas get it too.

1. Write the email, then choose **Repeat** in the Send panel
2. Choose **Every month** (recommended, because report figures change monthly) or **Every week**
3. Pick the date and time of the first send

Your repeating emails show as cards at the top of **Sent & scheduled**, each marked **On** or **Paused**, with who it goes to, when it sends next and how many it has sent so far. Each card has **Edit**, to change the email on its own page, **Pause** to stop it for a while (**Resume** starts it again), and a bin to delete it.

A repeating email is changed on a computer. On your phone, its card still pauses, resumes or deletes it.

The **Month** merge field always shows the month the email goes out.

A repeating email keeps its report up to date by itself. If the email shows figures from a report made in an earlier month, the report is updated a couple of hours before the send, so every email carries that month's figures. It uses the usual report credits, at most once a month for each report, and you won't get a "report ready" email for it.

If there aren't enough credits to send, or to update its report, that one send is skipped rather than going out with old figures. The email stays on, and the next one goes as planned. We email you when a send is skipped (while **Campaign completed** is on), and it shows in **Sent** marked **Skipped**. Top up before the next send so it goes.

If your plan no longer includes emails to leads, each send is skipped, and the email carries on by itself once your plan does again.

A repeating email pauses itself only for something that needs you: for example, its report is no longer shared, or its area is outside your patch. It shows why on its card, and we email you (while **Campaign completed** is on). Put it right, then press **Resume**, and it goes at its next send time.

## Templates

Templates on **Drafts & templates** come in two groups: **Your templates**, which you make and edit, and **Market Trak templates**, which we write and keep up to date. Click one to start a new email from it. The buttons on the right of each row:

- the eye shows it large, with **Use this template** under it
- the pencil (**Edit**) opens one of your templates to change it
- **Duplicate** makes your own copy, of one of ours or one of yours, to change as you like
- the bin deletes one of your templates (or a draft)

An email you start from a template never changes the template. If you have edited a template and press **Use this template** before saving, you are asked whether your changes go into the template (**Save and use**) or only into this email (**This email only**).

To keep an email you have written for next time, press **Save as template** at the top of the email, on a computer. It shows once you have changed the words, or on a draft, since a template left as it is is already saved.

## Merge fields

Merge fields fill in each lead's own details when the email is sent. In the email they show as coloured labels. The assistant and older templates write them in curly brackets, as in the last column.

| Merge field | What it puts in | Written as |
| ----------- | --------------- | ---------- |
| **Personalise** | | |
| Agency name | Your agency name | `{{workspace_name}}` |
| Area | The report's area, or theirs | `{{location}}` |
| Booking link | Your calendar booking link. Blank if you have not saved one | `{{booking_link}}` |
| First name | Or "there" if we do not have one | `{{lead_name}}` |
| Month | This month, for example "September" | `{{current_date}}` |
| Sender name | The name your emails come from | `{{sender_name}}` |
| Website link | Your website, shown as its address, for example "www.youragency.co.uk". Blank if you have not saved one | `{{website_link}}` |
| **From the property** | | |

| Street and postcode | For example "Elm Road, M20" | `{{property_street}}` |
| **From the valuation** | | |
| Valuation address | The home they valued. Blank if they have none | `{{valuation_address}}` |
| Valuation link | A link to their valuation. Blank if they have none | `{{valuation_link}}` |
| Valuation price | Their valuation price. Blank if they have none | `{{valuation_price}}` |

Use **Website link** for a line such as "For our full list of homes, visit www.youragency.co.uk": it shows your website's address and opens the page you saved. If you have no website saved, the sentence holding it is left out, as it is for **Booking link**.

The report fields need a report chosen for the email. The **Insert** menu shows the same four groups in every email, each in alphabetical order, and only the fields that can be filled: **Booking link** and **Website link** only once you have saved them in **Settings > Branding** (your Calendar Booking Link and your Business Website), **From the valuation** once a lead has valued their home, and **From the property** once someone has asked about a property on one of your ads or your Bio Link. **Full address** and **Street and postcode** read "the property" for a lead who has not asked about one.

## On your phone

The same email builder works on your phone. It shows in one column: **Preview** and **Edit**, the email, then the Send panel (the **Send** button at the top takes you to it). **Choose an email** works the same way, with **Choose** on each row. On **Drafts & templates**, the eye shows an email and the bin deletes a draft or one of your templates.

Templates and repeating emails are changed on a computer. On your phone, a repeating email's card on **Sent & scheduled** still pauses, resumes or deletes it.

## Sending from the assistant

You can ask the assistant to write and send an email for you. It offers the same choices as the Send panel (all your areas, one or more areas, leads outside your areas, or one address) and can send now, later, or every week or month. It shows you the email, who it goes to, how many people and when, and asks before it sends anything. It sends through the same sender as this page, so the email appears in **Sent & scheduled** like any other. Anyone who has unsubscribed is left out, and if you give it the address of someone who has unsubscribed, it tells you. See [Creating Content](/help-center/ai-assistant/creating-content#sending-the-email).

## Automatic emails

The **Automatic** tab lists the emails that go by themselves: the thank-you after a Facebook or Instagram ad enquiry, the thank-you after a Bio Link enquiry, the welcome email and the instant valuation email. Each has its own on/off switch on its row. Click one to change it in the same editor as your templates, and go back to the system template at any time. See [Welcome Emails](/help-center/leads/welcome-emails#changing-an-automatic-email).

## Seeing how your emails did

**Sent** lists every email from the last 12 months, grouped by month. Each shows how many people it reached and what share of them opened it and clicked a link. Press an email sent from the Emails page or the assistant to see it exactly as it went out. Most older emails open too, shown from the template they were sent from as that template reads today, with the figures they went out with. An older email written from scratch rather than from a template can't be shown.

Automatic welcome emails are left out. They go to one lead at a time and are not emails you chose to send, so counting them would flatter your figures. They still show on each lead's own record.

### Open and click tracking

Every email records when a lead opens it, and every link records when it is clicked before taking the reader straight on to where it goes. You do not need to set anything up, your links work exactly as normal, and unsubscribe links are never tracked.

Links to your report (the report card's button and the **Report link** merge field) are also tagged `utm_source=email&utm_medium=campaign`, so a visit from one shows as coming from email in any analytics you have added to your reports, such as [Google Analytics](/help-center/settings/tracking-and-analytics).

Clicks are worth watching more than opens. Some mail apps open the tracking on the lead's behalf and others block it, so open figures are always a little blurred, whereas a click is a real person choosing to look at what you sent.

For more, go to **Analytics** under **Measure** in the sidebar and find the **How your emails land** card: your email opens for the period you pick, your open rate, and each email with its opens and clicks.

## When a lead replies

Emails are sent from Market Trak, but replies come to you, not to us. When someone replies to one of your emails, it arrives in your inbox, and hitting reply puts you straight back in touch with them.

A small number of mail systems answer automatically rather than sending a real reply, and those answers go to a hidden address email providers use for delivery reports. Market Trak picks those up and forwards them to you, so nothing gets lost. Empty automatic answers, such as out-of-office notices with no message in them, are left out.

<Callout kind="tip">
If you want replies to go somewhere other than your account email, such as a shared office inbox, fill in **Reply-To Email** in **Settings > Capturing leads > Email settings** (or press the cog on the Emails page). It applies to every email your leads get from you, with or without a custom sending domain, and automatic replies we forward go there too.
</Callout>

## Email limits

There is no monthly limit on how many emails you send. What your plan limits is how many contacts (leads) you can hold: see [Contact limits](/help-center/leads/managing-leads#contact-limits). If your plan uses credits, the Send panel shows what each send costs before it goes.

## Tips

- Add a report card so each email carries fresh figures for the area
- Use the **First name** merge field to make each email feel personal
- Keep subject lines under 60 characters
- Send a test to yourself and read it on your phone before sending
- A monthly repeating email keeps you in front of every lead without you having to remember
