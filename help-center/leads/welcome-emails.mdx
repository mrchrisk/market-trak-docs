Welcome emails are sent automatically when a new lead is captured in your workspace. They make a great first impression and let leads know who you are and what to expect.

## How it works

When a lead enters your workspace (from a report lead gate, valuation form, or manual entry), Market Trak can automatically send them a welcome email. This happens:

1. **On lead creation** - for manually added leads or CSV imports (if the welcome email checkbox is ticked when you import).

2. **The moment a lead signs up** - for leads captured via a public report unlock, an embed signup, or a report email signup. Submission and welcome email happen in a single step.

People who enquire through one of your [Facebook or Instagram property ads](/help-center/advertising/property-ad-launcher), or who register their interest in the property on your [Bio Link](/help-center/leads/bio-link), get a **thank-you email** about the property they asked about instead of the general welcome.

Instant valuation leads get a separate **branded valuation email** with a link back to their result, controlled by its own switch (see below).

The welcome email is sent as a one-off message, not as part of a campaign.

## Turning welcome emails on or off

Two switches in **Settings > Email settings** (under **Capturing leads**), in the **Welcome & follow-up** section at the top of the page, control what gets sent the moment a lead arrives. Both default to on and save automatically when you flip them.

- **Send a welcome email to new leads** - one switch for every kind of new lead. Each lead gets the email written for how they arrived: the welcome email for report unlocks, email signups and embeds, and the thank-you naming the home for ad and bio link enquiries. Turning it off stops all of them.
- **Send branded valuation email** - after an instant valuation, email the lead a branded link back to their result. It has its own switch because it is the figure they asked for, not a welcome. Turn this off if your CRM already sends one.

If your CRM already replies to one kind of lead, such as your ad enquiries, and you want only that kind to stay quiet, get in touch and we'll help.
</Callout>

## The welcome email template

Your workspace has a system-provided welcome email template. This template:

- Is pre-written with sensible defaults

- Uses basic personalisation variables

- Includes a required unsubscribe link for compliance

### Available variables

The welcome email supports a limited set of safe variables:

| Variable               | What it inserts                       |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| `{{lead_name}}`        | Lead's first name                     |
| `{{location}}`         | The location associated with the lead |
| `{{workspace_name}}`   | Your company name                     |
| `{{unsubscribe_link}}` | Unsubscribe link (required)           |

> **Note:** Report-specific variables (like `{{report_link}}` or `{{averagePrice}}`) are not available in welcome emails because the email is sent before a report context exists.

## Email delivery

Welcome emails are sent using your workspace's configured email domain. If you have a custom email domain set up, the welcome email comes from your domain. Otherwise, it uses the default Market Trak sending domain.

## Tips

- Keep your welcome email short and friendly

- Introduce your agency and what the lead can expect to receive

- Include your contact details so they know how to reach you

- The welcome email sets the tone for your relationship with the lead, make it count
