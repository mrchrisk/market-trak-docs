---
title: Welcome Emails
description: Automatically send a welcome email to new leads when they are captured.
---
Welcome emails are sent automatically when a new lead is captured in your workspace. They make a great first impression and let leads know who you are and what to expect.

## How it works

The welcome email goes out the moment a lead signs up, for leads captured through a public report unlock, an email signup form or an embed signup. Submission and welcome email happen in a single step.

For a lead you add yourself with **Add Lead**, the box on that form decides: tick **Send a welcome email to this lead** (on your phone, the **Send the welcome email** switch) and they get one.

People who enquire through one of your [Facebook or Instagram property ads](/help-center/advertising/property-ad-launcher), or who register their interest in the property on your [Bio Link](/help-center/leads/bio-link), get a **thank-you email** about the property they asked about instead of the general welcome.

Instant valuation leads don't get the welcome email. They get the **instant valuation email** with a button back to their result, which has its own switch (see below).

[Imported leads](/help-center/leads/importing-leads) are never sent a welcome email.

The welcome email is sent as a one-off message, not as part of a campaign.

## Turning them on or off

Go to **Emails**, then the **Automatic** tab. Each automatic email has its own switch on its row, so you can turn one off and leave the others on, for example if your CRM already replies to your ad enquiries:

- **Facebook and Instagram enquiry thank-you** - when someone enquires on one of your ads
- **Bio Link thank-you** - when someone registers their interest on your Bio Link
- **Welcome email** - when someone unlocks a report or signs up for updates, including through an embed
- **Instant valuation email** - when someone values their home on your valuation page. Turn this off if your CRM already sends one

They are all on to start with, and a switch saves as soon as you flip it. **Settings > Email settings** has a link to the same place.

## What they look like

All four look like the emails you send from the Emails page: your words, with your agency name and an unsubscribe link in the footer at the bottom. Like those emails, they have no logo unless you add one.

- **The thank-yous** thank the person for their enquiry and show the property they asked about, with its photo, price and a button to the listing, and your phone number if you have added one in your settings.
- **The welcome email** greets the lead by first name, thanks them for their interest in their area's property market and tells them what to expect from you. If they unlocked a report, a button to it sits just above your sign-off.
- **The instant valuation email** tells them their valuation is ready, with a button back to their result just above your sign-off.

Until you make your own version, each one goes out as the **system template**, which we keep up to date: when we improve one, your next email uses it straight away.

## Changing an automatic email

Click an email on the **Automatic** tab and it opens in the same editor as your templates, with the preview beside it filled with example details.

1. Change the **Subject**, the **Preview line** and the words, and add pictures, buttons or anything else from the toolbar, just as in a template
2. Type **/** or press **Insert** to add a detail that changes for each person. Each email offers only the details it can fill, such as **Property address**, **Property street and postcode** and **Your phone number** in the thank-yous, or **Area** in the welcome email. If you have no phone number saved, the sentence with it is left out
3. Press **Save**. The next one goes out exactly as you saved it

Each email has its own block: the **property card** in the thank-yous, the **report button** in the welcome email and the **valuation button** in the instant valuation email. You can drag it to where you want it. If you delete the property card or the report button, a button on the toolbar puts it back. The valuation button can't be deleted, because it is what that email is for.

To add your logo, press **Image** on the toolbar and pick one of your saved logos under **Or add your logo**. Choose the one you can see clearly, since emails are white. It goes in small, like a letterhead, and you can drag it to the top.

Each email has a badge on the tab: **Your version** once you have saved your own, or **System template** until then. To go back to the system template, open the email, press **Restore system template** and confirm.

## Email delivery

Welcome emails are sent using your workspace's configured email domain. If you have a custom email domain set up, the welcome email comes from your domain. Otherwise, it uses the default Market Trak sending domain.
