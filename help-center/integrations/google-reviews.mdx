---
title: "Turn your Google reviews into posts"
description: "Connect your Google Business listing so your newest reviews arrive in your Daily Swipes as finished, branded posts."
---

Your Google reviews are the most persuasive thing anyone writes about you. Connect your listing once and your newest review turns up in your Daily Swipes as a finished post: your customer's words on a branded image in your colours, with a caption already written underneath.

There is no Google login involved. You point at your business, we do the rest.

<Callout kind="info">
Review Posts is included on the **Premium** and **Ultimate** plans.
</Callout>

## Connect your listing

<Steps>
<Step title="Open your integrations" icon="settings" titleType="p">
Go to **Workspace Settings**, then the **Integrations** tab, and open **Google Reviews**. On a phone, tap your initials in the top right, then **Integrations**, then **Google Reviews**.
</Step>
<Step title="Find your business" icon="search" titleType="p">
Press **Search**. We look your business up on Google using the name and website already on your account, so most of the time you can leave the box empty.

If nothing useful comes back, the surest fix is your Google link: find your business on Google, copy the page link from the address bar, and paste it into the search box. Typing your business name and your town, exactly as they appear on your Google listing, works too.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose which reviews become posts" icon="star" titleType="p">
Pick **Five stars only**, or **Four and five stars**. Five stars only is the default and is what we recommend: a four-star review often carries a "but" in the second sentence.

You can change this later by disconnecting and connecting again.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm it is you" icon="check" titleType="p">
Press **This is us** next to the right listing. Check the address, not just the name, if there is more than one business with a similar name near you.
</Step>
</Steps>

Once you are connected we take your five most recent reviews straight away, so there is usually something waiting for you the same day.

## What happens next

We check for new reviews once a week. When one arrives that meets your star rating, it appears in your **Daily Swipes** as a review card.

Every review card shows:

- Your customer's words on a branded image in your colours
- Their name, and "Google review" underneath it
- Their star rating
- A caption alongside, written about what they said

Keep it and it goes to your calendar like any other post. Pass and it is gone.

<Callout kind="tip">
A review card is always the first card in your deck, so you will see it as soon as you open your swipes.
</Callout>

## What we never do

We do not reword what your customer wrote. Their words go on the image exactly as they left them, which is the whole reason a review is worth reading.

If a review is long, we use the opening sentences rather than shortening it in the middle, so what appears is always something they actually wrote.

## Common questions

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How many posts will I get?" default-open="false">
One per review. If you receive two reviews a month, you will get two review posts a month.

When you first connect we take your five most recent reviews, so there is a small batch to begin with. After that it follows whatever comes in.

A review is only ever posted once. On a day with nothing new, your deck simply has one fewer card rather than repeating a review you have already used.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Why has no review card appeared?" default-open="false">
The most likely reason is that none of your recent reviews meet your star rating. If you chose **Five stars only** and your recent reviews are four stars, nothing will come through. Reconnect and choose **Four and five stars** if you would like those included.

The other reason is that we have already used them. Reviews are posted once each, so once your five are through, the next card waits for a new review.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I use reviews from Facebook or a portal?" default-open="false">
Not at the moment. Review Posts reads your Google Business listing only.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="My business does not come up in the search" default-open="false">
Paste your Google link instead: search for your business on Google, copy the link from the address bar, and paste it into the search box. That works with Google Maps links and share links too, and it is the most reliable way to find a listing the name search misses.

If a pasted link still finds nothing, check that your Google Business Profile is live by searching for your business on Google Maps. If it does not show there, it will not show here.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What happens if I disconnect?" default-open="false">
We stop checking for new reviews and no further review cards appear.

Posts you have already published are not affected, and reconnecting later will not repeat a review you have already used.
</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
