Once you've made your first Content Pack, you can try everything Market Trak does, free for 14 days. There is no card to enter and no checkout to go through: one click unlocks every feature, and when your free days end your account simply returns to the free plan.

<Callout kind="info">
The trial is completely free. Nothing is charged, there is no payment method on file, and there is nothing to cancel. If you do nothing, nothing happens except your account returning to the free plan.
</Callout>

## Starting your trial

<Steps>
<Step title="Make your first Content Pack" icon="wand-2" titleType="p">
Sign up and complete the welcome flow. Market Trak builds your first Content Pack: a market report for your area plus six ready-to-post social posts.
</Step>
<Step title="Click the unlock button" icon="sparkles" titleType="p">
On your completed pack (or on any locked feature around the app), click **Unlock everything free for 14 days**.
</Step>
<Step title="That's it" icon="check" titleType="p">
Your account unlocks on the spot. No card, no forms, no confirmation emails to wait for.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Getting set up after you unlock

As soon as your trial starts, one screen asks for the single connection that makes everything else work: **your Facebook Page**. Connect it and every post you approve goes straight onto your calendar and publishes itself, on Facebook and on the Instagram account linked to your Page. You can press "I'll do this later" instead; either way you'll be offered a free setup call, where we can make the connection with you if you'd rather not do it alone.

Your **properties** usually need nothing from you at all: we use the website address you gave when you signed up to find your listings automatically. If yours don't appear, the setup guide in your sidebar has a step to connect your website (or CRM) yourself.

The connect screen only appears once. After that, your Content Pack shows a reminder strip until your Page is connected, a short tour points out how the pack works the first time you see one, and the sidebar setup guide lists anything still outstanding.

## What the trial includes

Every feature Market Trak has, for 14 days, on the Premium plan's allowances:

- **Content Packs** for your postcode areas, whenever you need them
- **Publishing and scheduling** to Facebook and Instagram
- **The AI Assistant** for posts, emails and canvassing letters
- **Your online valuation tool** and live report pages, capturing seller leads
- **Email campaigns** to your leads
- **Property Packs** that turn one listing into a set of branded posts
- **The Ad Launcher** for Facebook and Instagram property ads, which normally sits on our top plan
- A generous allowance of AI credits to power it all

Your trial runs on Premium allowances rather than our biggest plan, so the postcode areas and contacts you get used to are the ones you keep if you subscribe to Premium. The Ad Launcher is the exception and is switched on for every trial. Adding team members is the one thing a trial cannot do.

## When your trial ends

Your account returns to the free plan, and **everything you made stays yours**: your posts, reports, packs and leads all remain visible in your account.

What changes on the free plan:

- Actions that create or send things (publishing, new packs, campaigns, the assistant) show an upgrade prompt instead of running
- Your public report and valuation pages go private, so links you shared stop showing content until you're back on a plan
- **Posts already scheduled for a date after your trial ends stay on your calendar but don't publish.** This is deliberate: the report and valuation links inside those posts go private at the same moment, so publishing them would send homeowners to pages they can't see
- Any Facebook ads still running through Market Trak are paused so nothing spends while you can't manage it

Choose a plan at any time and it all switches back on, exactly where you left off. Scheduled posts that were waiting go out on the dates you originally set.

### Seeing what it costs, during the trial

You do not have to wait until the end, or hunt for a locked button. Open the **settings menu** at the bottom-left of the app and choose **Pricing**, and every plan appears side by side with monthly and yearly options. You can also ask the AI Assistant what it costs, or open [marketrak.pro/pricing](https://marketrak.pro/pricing) in a tab.

One thing worth knowing: a post whose date has already gone by more than a day won't publish late. It waits on your calendar marked as on hold, so a week of posts doesn't suddenly go out at once the moment you subscribe. Give it a new date and it publishes as normal.

<Callout kind="tip">
You'll get a reminder email the day before your trial ends, so it never catches you by surprise. If you have posts scheduled beyond that date, the email tells you how many, and your dashboard shows the same reminder afterwards.
</Callout>

## Common questions

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Do I need to enter a card?" default-open="false">
No. The trial never asks for a payment method. You only enter a card if you decide to subscribe to a plan.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Will I be charged when the trial ends?" default-open="false">
No. There is nothing on file to charge. Your account simply returns to the free plan.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Do I lose my work when the trial ends?" default-open="false">
No. Everything you made stays in your account. You just can't make more or send things out until you choose a plan.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What happens to posts I've already scheduled?" default-open="false">
They stay on your calendar, but any dated after your trial ends won't publish while you're on the free plan. We tell you how many, in the reminder email the day before and on your dashboard afterwards. Choose a plan and they publish on the dates you set. If a post's date has already passed by more than a day, it waits on your calendar rather than going out late: give it a new date and it goes as normal. Nothing is deleted in the meantime.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I have another trial later?" default-open="false">
The trial is one per workspace. After it ends, the plans on the [Pricing page](https://marketrak.pro/pricing) are the way to keep going.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What happens to posts I scheduled during the trial?" default-open="false">
Scheduled posts don't go out on the free plan. They stay on your calendar, and scheduling picks back up when you subscribe.
</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
