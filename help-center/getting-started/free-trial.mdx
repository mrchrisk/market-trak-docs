title: "Your 14-Day Free Trial"
description: "Unlock the whole of Market Trak free for 14 days with one click. No card, no checkout, nothing to cancel."
---
Once you've made your first Content Pack, you can try everything Market Trak does, free for 14 days. There is no card to enter and no checkout to go through: one click unlocks the full platform, and when your free days end your account simply returns to the free plan.

<Callout kind="info">
The trial is completely free. Nothing is charged, there is no payment method on file, and there is nothing to cancel. If you do nothing, nothing happens except your account returning to the free plan.
</Callout>

## Starting your trial

<Steps>
<Step title="Make your first Content Pack" icon="wand-2" titleType="p">
Sign up and complete the welcome flow. Market Trak builds your first Content Pack: a market report for your area plus six ready-to-post social posts.
</Step>
<Step title="Click the unlock button" icon="sparkles" titleType="p">
On your completed pack (or on any locked feature around the app), click **Unlock everything free for 14 days**.
</Step>
<Step title="That's it" icon="check" titleType="p">
Your account unlocks on the spot. No card, no forms, no confirmation emails to wait for.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Getting set up after you unlock

As soon as your trial starts, a short setup guide appears and walks you through the two connections that make everything else work:

1. **Your Facebook Page**, so Market Trak can publish and schedule your posts for you, on Facebook and on the Instagram account linked to your Page.
2. **Your property feed**, which brings your listings in from your website or CRM so they're ready for posts, Property Packs and ads. New listings then arrive on their own.

You can skip either step and come back to it later. If you go off to connect something, the guide picks up where you left off as soon as you're done, and it finishes on a short reminder of the weekly rhythm that gets the best out of Market Trak. It only appears once; after that, the setup guide in your sidebar shows anything still outstanding.

## What the trial includes

Everything on our top plan, for 14 days:

- **Content Packs** for any postcode you cover, whenever you need them
- **Publishing and scheduling** to Facebook and Instagram
- **The AI Assistant** for posts, emails and canvassing letters
- **Your online valuation tool** and live report pages, capturing seller leads
- **Email campaigns** to your leads
- **Property Packs** that turn one listing into a set of branded posts
- **The Ad Launcher** for Facebook and Instagram property ads
- A generous allowance of AI credits to power it all

## When your trial ends

Your account returns to the free plan, and **everything you made stays yours**: your posts, reports, packs and leads all remain visible in your account.

What changes on the free plan:

- Actions that create or send things (publishing, new packs, campaigns, the assistant) show an upgrade prompt instead of running
- Your public report and valuation pages go private, so links you shared stop showing content until you're back on a plan
- Any Facebook ads still running through Market Trak are paused so nothing spends while you can't manage it

Choose a plan at any time and it all switches back on, exactly where you left off.

<Callout kind="tip">
You'll get a reminder email the day before your trial ends, so it never catches you by surprise.
</Callout>

## Common questions

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Do I need to enter a card?" default-open="false">
No. The trial never asks for a payment method. You only enter a card if you decide to subscribe to a plan.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Will I be charged when the trial ends?" default-open="false">
No. There is nothing on file to charge. Your account simply returns to the free plan.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Do I lose my work when the trial ends?" default-open="false">
No. Everything you made stays in your account. You just can't make more or send things out until you choose a plan.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I have another trial later?" default-open="false">
The trial is one per workspace. After it ends, the plans on the [Pricing page](https://marketrak.pro/pricing) are the way to keep going.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What happens to posts I scheduled during the trial?" default-open="false">
Scheduled posts don't go out on the free plan. They stay on your calendar, and scheduling picks back up when you subscribe.
</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
