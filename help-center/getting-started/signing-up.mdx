Getting started with Market Trak is fast. You sign up with just your email, set a password, and we deliver your first Magic Pack so you can see exactly what the platform produces for your area before deciding to subscribe.

## Creating your account

<Steps>
<Step title="Enter your email" icon="mail" titleType="p">
Go to [marketrak.pro](https://marketrak.pro) and click **Get Started**. Enter your name and email address. No card needed.
</Step>
<Step title="Open the email we send you" icon="inbox" titleType="p">
Check your inbox for a confirmation email from Market Trak and click the link inside. This verifies your email and signs you into your new account.
</Step>
<Step title="Set your password" icon="lock" titleType="p">
You land on a page where you choose a password for your account.
</Step>
<Step title="Tell us about your business" icon="building" titleType="p">
Enter your agency website. We scrape it for your branding (logo, colours, business name) so your Magic Pack is on brand from the very first post.
</Step>
<Step title="See your first Magic Pack" icon="sparkles" titleType="p">
Within seconds, three social media posts and a market intelligence report for your area are delivered to your workspace, fully branded. This is your free preview of what Market Trak generates.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Your free Magic Pack

Every new account includes one free Magic Pack. You can view the posts, read the report, and see exactly the quality of content Market Trak produces for your local area, all without entering a card.

To publish or schedule the posts, edit images and captions, regenerate the pack, or unlock the full platform (lead capture, email campaigns, additional reports, the AI assistant, and more), you need to subscribe.

<Callout kind="info">
The free Magic Pack is a one-time preview per workspace, so make sure the postcode you enter on the website-scrape step is the one you most want to see content for.
</Callout>

## Signing in

Already have an account? Go to [marketrak.pro/auth](https://marketrak.pro/auth) and sign in with your email and password.

### Forgot your password?

On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?** to receive a password reset email. Follow the link to set a new password.

## Next steps

Once you have explored your Magic Pack and subscribed, follow the [First Steps](/help-center/getting-started/first-steps) guide to finish setting up your workspace.
