Getting started with Market Trak is fast. You sign up with just your email, set a password, and we deliver your first Content Pack so you can see exactly what the platform produces for your area before deciding to subscribe.

## Creating your account

You can sign up with Google for the fastest start, or use email and password. Either route lands you on the same workspace setup screen.

### Sign up with Google (fastest)

Go to [marketrak.pro](https://marketrak.pro), click **Get Started**, then click **Continue with Google** at the top of the signup card. Choose your Google account and you'll skip straight to the workspace setup screen, no email confirmation step required. Your name and email come across from Google automatically.

<Callout kind="info">
The very first time you sign in with Google, you may see a "Google hasn't verified this app" screen. This is normal while Market Trak is being reviewed by Google and the warning will disappear once verification clears. You can safely continue past it.
</Callout>

### Sign up with email and password

<Steps>
<Step title="Enter your email" icon="mail" titleType="p">
Go to [marketrak.pro](https://marketrak.pro) and click **Get Started**. Enter your name and email address. No card needed.
</Step>
<Step title="Open the email we send you" icon="inbox" titleType="p">
Check your inbox for a confirmation email from Market Trak and click the link inside. This verifies your email and signs you into your new account.
</Step>
<Step title="Set your password" icon="lock" titleType="p">
You land on a page where you choose a password for your account.
</Step>
<Step title="Tell us about your business" icon="building" titleType="p">
Enter your agency website. We scrape it for your branding (logo, colours, business name) so your Content Pack is on brand from the very first post.
</Step>
<Step title="See your first Content Pack" icon="sparkles" titleType="p">
Within seconds, three social media posts and a market intelligence report for your area are delivered to your workspace, fully branded. This is your free preview of what Market Trak generates.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Your free Content Pack

Every new account includes one free Content Pack. You can view the posts, read the report, and see exactly the quality of content Market Trak produces for your local area, all without entering a card.

You can publish your pack's posts to your Facebook Page during onboarding for free; a small #MarketTrak hashtag is added to those posts. To edit captions and images, schedule posts, generate more packs, or unlock the full platform (lead capture, email campaigns, additional reports, the AI assistant, and more), you need to subscribe.

When you tap any locked action, Market Trak shows you what a subscription unlocks, and from there you can compare every plan side by side on a full-screen plans page, with monthly and yearly pricing. You can return to your pack at any time with the **X** button in the top-right corner.

<Callout kind="info">
The free Content Pack is a one-time preview per workspace, so make sure the postcode you enter on the website-scrape step is the one you most want to see content for.
</Callout>

## Signing in

Already have an account? Go to [marketrak.pro/auth](https://marketrak.pro/auth) and either click **Continue with Google** (if you signed up with Google) or sign in with your email and password.

If you originally signed up with email and password but would prefer to use Google going forward, click **Continue with Google** with the same Google account that owns your signup email. Market Trak will automatically link the two so you keep the same workspace and history — no duplicate account.

### Forgot your password?

On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?** to receive a password reset email. Follow the link to set a new password. If you signed up with Google you don't have a Market Trak password — use the Continue with Google button instead.

## Next steps

Once you have explored your Content Pack and subscribed, follow the [First Steps](/help-center/getting-started/first-steps) guide to finish setting up your workspace.
