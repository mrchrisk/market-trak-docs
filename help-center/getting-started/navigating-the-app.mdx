Market Trak has a clean, sidebar-driven layout that keeps every feature one click away.

## Sidebar navigation

The sidebar on the left is your main navigation. **Dashboard** and the **AI Assistant** sit at the top, and the rest of your tools are grouped to follow your workflow: **Create** your content, **Share** it, then **Nurture** the people it brings in.

| Group   | Item                 | Description                                                       |
| ------- | -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Top     | **Dashboard**        | Your command centre with metrics, awareness score, and quick wins |
| Top     | **AI Assistant**     | Chat with your assistant for content creation and CRM queries     |
| Create  | **Magic Packs**      | Generate a ready-to-use set of posts plus a report for any area   |
| Create  | **Reports**          | Generate, view, edit, and share property market reports           |
| Create  | **Social Posts**     | Create and manage your social media content                       |
| Share   | **Content Calendar** | Plan and schedule your posts across the week and month            |
| Share   | **Ad Launcher**      | Launch a Facebook or Instagram lead ad for a property             |
| Nurture | **Leads**            | Manage captured leads and run email campaigns                     |

Some items may show a lock icon if they need a higher subscription tier, and the **Ad Launcher** appears once advertising is enabled for your workspace.

### Collapsing the sidebar

Click **Collapse** at the top of the sidebar (next to the logo)to shrink it to icons only. Click the expand arrow to bring it back. Your preference is remembered.

## Profile menu

Click your **profile icon** at the bottom of the sidebar to open a popover with:

- **User settings** - personal profile and notification preferences

- **Workspace settings** - branding, team, and email configuration

- **Billing** - subscription management and credit purchases

- **Get help** - opens live chat support

- **Sign out**

## Mobile navigation

On mobile devices, the sidebar is replaced by:

- A **top header** with your logo and menu

- A **bottom navigation bar** with the main sections (Dashboard, Reports, Social, Leads)

The experience is fully responsive, so every feature works on your phone or tablet.

## Locked features

If a sidebar item shows a lock icon, it means your current plan does not include that feature. Click through to see upgrade options, or visit [Billing](/help-center/settings/billing) to change your plan.
