title: Your First Welcome Flow
description: What to expect the first time you sign in. Paste your website, review your branding, and generate your first Magic pack.
---
The very first time you sign in to a new Market Trak workspace, you go through a short full-screen welcome experience that gets your branding set up and produces your first Magic pack before you ever see the main dashboard.

The whole thing takes around two minutes. You can't skip past it on your first visit, by design. By the time you reach the dashboard, your workspace is already branded and you have your first Magic pack to use.

## Step 1: Paste your agency website

You'll see a single field asking for your agency website. Paste in your full URL (the same one your customers visit) and click **Find my brand**.

If you'd rather skip this and enter everything manually, you can click **Skip for now** below the input. Most people are better off pasting the URL, Market Trak does the work for you.

## Step 2: We read your site

While you wait, Market Trak visits your website and pulls out three pieces of information:

- Your **logo**

- Your **primary brand colour**

- Your **agency name**

This usually takes 5 to 15 seconds.

## Step 3: Review what we found

You'll see three editable cards with what we pulled from your site. Anything we got wrong, you can fix here before moving on:

- **Agency name**: type over what we found if it isn't quite right.

- **Brand colour**: click the swatch to open a colour picker, type a hex code directly, or click one of the four suggested shades next to it (slightly lighter or darker variants of what we detected).

- **Logo**: we show the logo we picked from your site. If you'd rather use a different one, drag a file onto the **Upload your own** tile (or click it to open a file picker). PNG, JPG, SVG, or WebP up to 2MB.

If we couldn't read your site (rare, usually because your site blocks automated visits or didn't load in time), you'll land on this step with empty fields and a small note. Just type in your agency name, pick a colour, and upload your logo manually.

When everything looks right, click **Looks good, let's build your first pack**.

## Step 4: Your first Magic pack

We save your branding to your workspace and drop you straight onto a full-screen Magic Generate canvas. Type in a postcode you want to target, click **Generate**, and Market Trak builds:

- A local market report for that postcode (with your logo and brand colour applied)

- Three social posts in different visual styles

This usually takes around 90 seconds. The logo and brand colour you just set are already applied to the report.

When the pack lands, you'll see a small **Welcome to Market Trak** banner at the top, a confetti burst, and a **Continue to your dashboard** button at the bottom of the canvas. Click it and the full app opens up.

## After the welcome flow

From this point on, signing in takes you straight to the dashboard like normal. Your first report and three posts are saved automatically:

- The report lives on your **Reports** page

- The three social posts live in your **Drafts** under Social Media

You can edit any of them, post or schedule them, or generate more packs at any time from the **Magic** page in the sidebar.

## What if I want to change my branding later?

Everything you set during the welcome flow can be edited later from **Workspace Settings > Branding**. Your logo, brand colour, and agency name are all editable at any time.
