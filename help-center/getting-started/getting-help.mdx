There are two ways to reach the Market Trak team: **live chat** inside the app, or **email**.

## Live chat

Chat is the quickest way to reach us. You open it from the menu.

<Tabs>
<Tab title="On a computer" icon="monitor">
1. Click your **profile icon** at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Click **Get help**.

The chat opens on the page you are on. You can also find **Get help** in **Settings**, under **Account**.
</Tab>
<Tab title="On your phone" icon="smartphone">
1. Tap **your initials** in the top-right corner to open **Account**.
2. Tap **Get help**, in the **Account** group.

The chat opens over the screen you are on.
</Tab>
</Tabs>

When we reply, a number appears on **Get help** showing how many messages you have not read yet.

## Email

Email us at **support@marketrak.pro**.

To help us sort things out quickly, include:

- the name of your agency or workspace
- what you were trying to do, and what happened instead
- a screenshot, if you can take one

## How-to questions

For questions about how something works, you can also ask the [Assistant](/help-center/ai-assistant/overview) inside the app, or search this help centre. If the answer is not here, use chat or email and we will help.
