The **Property Pack** page turns one property into a week and a half of social content. Pick a property, check its photos and description, choose the occasion (Just listed, Open house, Sold and more), and about a minute later you have six designed posts. Each one uses a different photo with your agency's logo, brand colour and the property's price genuinely built into the image itself, not laid over it in the app, so the branding is part of the picture wherever it gets shared.

Find it in the sidebar under **Property Pack**, just below Content Pack. Every pack you generate is also kept in your **Library** (the **Property Packs** tab), so you can come back for another post about a property weeks later, and delete the pack once the property is gone; posts you've already scheduled or published stay where they are.

<Callout kind="info">
Property Packs are the Content Pack's sibling. A Content Pack markets an **area** from live market data; a Property Pack markets **one property** from its own photos and your listing description.
</Callout>

## What's in a pack

Six post drafts, whatever you give it: add one photo and your best shots return in different designs; add six and every post gets its own. A typical pack mixes:

- An **announcement post** on your lead photo, carrying the price, the occasion and the address on a designed price card
- Question and statement cards, quotes over the photo, a gallery-print look, and occasion ribbons or badges, so the six posts never feel like the same design repeated
- A **photo collage** under your occasion banner when the property has four or more photos

Every caption is written from your own listing description, and each one is matched to its photo: the line about the log burner sits on the photo showing the log burner, not the garden. Captions end with a call to action (message the agency, comment for details, share it, save it, or view the listing), varied across the set.

Posts arrive with suggested publish dates spread **every other day**, so one property fills around twelve days of your calendar rather than crowding a single week.

## How to generate a pack

<Steps>
<Step title="Pick your property" icon="home" titleType="p">
The page opens on your property grid, the same catalogue the Ad Launcher uses. Everything is in one grid, newest first: your portfolio synced from your connected CRM (Loop or Street) or your website's property feed (sold CRM listings drop off automatically), plus anything you've added by hand. Click **Add property** to paste a listing's web address and Market Trak reads the details for you. A small filter above the grid narrows it to one source if you want.

No listing anywhere yet? Coming-soon stock works too: use **Start with your own photos** under the grid and upload them yourself.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose the photos" icon="image" titleType="p">
The property's full photo set appears with the best six already selected. Scroll through the strip (use the arrows or swipe), tap any photo to swap it in or out, and add your own with **Add photos**, by clicking it or by dragging files onto it. Six photos is the maximum; floor plans and EPC charts are filtered out for you.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the details" icon="list-checks" titleType="p">
The description, price and address arrive filled in from the property you picked, ready to correct rather than type. Pick the **Occasion** (it drives the price card's tag and the ribbon and badge designs), and check the **Listing link**: when the property has a public listing page the link is filled in for you, and you can paste one or leave it empty.
</Step>
<Step title="Generate" icon="sparkles" titleType="p">
Click **Generate my Property Pack**. The progress screen tracks the real stages (writing your captions, branding your photos, wrapping up) and a pack usually lands in under a minute. Feel free to move around the app; if you come back mid-build you'll rejoin the progress screen, and if it finished while you were away you'll land straight on the results.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Reviewing your pack

The review works like a Content Pack week:

- **Date chips**: every post carries its suggested publish date, every other day at a good posting time, picked around whatever is already on your calendar so nothing clashes. Tap the chip to change the day or time; a date that has already passed turns red and needs a new date before approving. Times you set yourself are never changed.
- **Thumbs up**: schedules that post to its date on your connected channels, with **Publish now instead** and **Change date** underneath once approved.
- **Thumbs down**: removes the post from the pack. Unlike Content Packs there's no rebuild for the slot, because the pack is built from the property's fixed photo set; the rest of the pack stays as it is.
- **Approve the week**: one button schedules every post you haven't actioned, each to its own date, after confirming which channels to post to. If some of the planned dates have already passed, the dialog tells you and the button becomes **Update dates and approve**: your posts get fresh dates, one every other day starting tomorrow.
- **Edit first if you like**: the pencil edits the caption and hashtags, and hovering the image lets you replace it or open the AI image editor.

<Callout kind="info">
Approved and published posts are yours forever. Posts you never action are cleared 30 days after the pack was generated.
</Callout>

## Where the links in captions go

- With a **listing link** on the pack, the first and last captions invite readers to view the full listing at that link.
- Without one (coming-soon stock, or a CRM property with no public page), the final caption points readers to your instant valuation page instead, so the pack still captures leads.

## Cost and availability

Property Packs are available on paid plans. A pack uses credits from your balance, charged once per pack and only when your posts are delivered; a failed generation costs nothing.

## Troubleshooting

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="A photo looks broken or missing in the picker" default-open="false">
Photos that fail to load are removed from the strip automatically. You can always add your own with **Add photos**; uploads work exactly like listing photos in the pack.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="My pasted link brought in the description but few photos" default-open="false">
Most listing pages work, including sites that build their photo gallery with JavaScript. If a site still gives up very few photos, upload the ones you want alongside; the pack treats them the same.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="It says my description is too short" default-open="false">
Captions are written from your description, so a few sentences is the minimum. Paste your listing description, or write a short paragraph about the property's best features.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="I got fewer than six posts" default-open="false">
Very occasionally one image can't be branded even after retries, and the pack ships without that slot rather than including an unbranded post. Generating again usually returns the full six.
</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
