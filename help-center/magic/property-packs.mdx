title: "Property Packs"

---

A Property Pack is the Content Pack's sibling for a single property. Give it your listing (or just your photos and description) and every photo becomes a social post draft with your branding on the image itself: your logo, your brand colour, the price, and a status tag like "Coming soon", all part of the stored picture, not a preview effect. Captions are written from your own description, each post taking a different angle, so the set never repeats itself.

<Callout kind="info">
Property Packs are available on paid plans. Find them under **Create > Property Packs** in the menu.
</Callout>

## Two ways to start

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Paste your listing link" icon="link">
Paste the property's page from your own website (portal links work too) and tap **Fetch details**. Market Trak reads the page and fills in the description, price, and address for you, and offers the photos it found so you can tap the ones you want to use. Check everything, adjust anything, then generate.
</Tab>
<Tab title="Build it yourself" icon="image-plus">
No link yet? Perfect for coming-soon properties that aren't listed anywhere. Upload 3 to 6 photos, paste in the listing description you've written, set the price (or leave it blank to show POA), pick a status like **Coming soon**, and generate.
</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Creating a pack

<Steps>
<Step title="Add the property" icon="home" titleType="p">
Paste a listing link and fetch the details, or upload photos and paste your description. Six photos fills the pack best: you get one post per photo, laid out three to a row.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the details" icon="list-checks" titleType="p">
The price, status, and area you enter are placed onto the lead photo exactly as written, so give them a quick check. The description is the raw material for every caption; the more it says, the better the posts.
</Step>
<Step title="Generate" icon="sparkles" titleType="p">
Tap **Generate my Property Pack**. It usually takes a minute or two: captions are written first, then your branding is applied to each photo.
</Step>
<Step title="Review and schedule" icon="thumbs-up" titleType="p">
Each post arrives with a suggested publish date across the coming week. Thumbs up a post to approve and schedule it in one tap, edit any caption or photo first if you like, or use **Approve the week** to schedule everything at once. It's the same review flow as Content Packs.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What lands on your photos

- **The lead photo** carries a caption strip along the bottom with the price (or POA), the area and key facts, and your agency's logo, plus a small status tag such as JUST LISTED or COMING SOON in your brand colour.
- **Every other photo** carries one short highlight from your description ("South-facing garden", "Recently refitted kitchen") in a neat chip, with your logo in the corner.

Your logo and brand colour come from **Workspace Settings > Branding**. If no suitable logo is saved, your agency name is used as a clean wordmark instead.

<Callout kind="tip">
The branding is genuinely part of the image file, so it survives downloads, re-uploads, and scheduling. What you see on the card is exactly what publishes.
</Callout>

## Pricing

A Property Pack is one flat charge (50 credits on credits-based plans) covering the captions and all the branded photos, charged only when the pack completes successfully. On plans with monthly allowances there is no per-pack charge.

## Troubleshooting

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="The listing link couldn't be read" default-open="false">
Some websites block automated reading. You can always build the pack manually: upload the photos and paste the description straight in; the result is identical.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Some photos from my listing look wrong or are missing" default-open="false">
The photo picker shows what your listing page exposes, which sometimes includes odd images or misses a few. Untick anything you don't want and upload the missing ones from your computer; you can mix fetched and uploaded photos freely.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="The price or address looks wrong on the photo" default-open="false">
They're placed exactly as written in the Price and Area fields, so fix the text there and generate again. Leaving the price blank shows POA.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="A post's photo didn't appear" default-open="false">
If one photo can't be processed (for example, the file is corrupt or its web address stopped working), that post is left out rather than posted unbranded, and the rest of the pack completes normally.
</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
