**Swipes** gives you ready-to-use posts about your patch and listings. Keep a post to schedule it, Save it to your Library, or Pass. Meaningful corrections help improve future posts across Market Trak.

Find **Swipes** in the sidebar's Create group. On a phone, tap **Content**, then **Swipes**.

## Your suggestions and where kept posts go

Eligible plans include up to **15 new suggestions in seven days**, delivered in batches of up to **five**. Suggestions and their images do not use your credits. Free accounts do not receive new decks; trials receive eligible plan access. Administrator-set allowances can differ.

When you keep a post, Swipes looks at everything already planned for the next seven days, including Content Packs and Property Packs, and puts it on the quietest day. Kept posts normally go out within a week, so their figures stay current. The second week is only used when the first has no room at all.

A busy calendar does not stop new Swipes being made. Your batch and weekly allowance are the only limit.

Batches are made on demand. Returning users can have a batch start when they open the app. Trial reminders may also prepare a batch, provided there are no waiting cards and your allowance permits it.

## Keep, Save and Pass

- **Keep** puts the post on your calendar. Check the proposed date above the card first. Automatic dates go to the quietest of the next seven days, weekends included, at least 90 minutes from any other post and never more than six posts on a day. If another post takes the slot before you keep it, the date is rechecked.
- **Save** puts the post in your Library without a date.
- **Pass** removes the suggestion from your deck.

On a phone, swipe right to Keep or left to Pass. The buttons work on every screen. Edit the words or replace the image before keeping or saving a post.

If you have no usable Facebook connection, Keep saves the post to the Library and tells you. If the next two weeks are completely full, Keep saves the post to the Library and tells you, or you can choose your own date. A time you choose yourself must be at least 30 minutes ahead.

**Where did this come from?** shows the source area and period for a market-data card. Advice cards without a figure are identified as opinions. Missing market figures are left out rather than presented as zero.

## Changing your mind

Today's decisions appear beneath the deck. **Put it back in the deck** undoes the decision and withdraws its learning evidence. If the post is still pending on your calendar, it is removed from the calendar too, including a saved post you later scheduled from the Library. A saved Library copy is withdrawn to avoid duplicates when you keep it again.

Once publishing has started, the post cannot be put back. A card already resolved through the approval queue cannot be restored over that separate decision.

You do not have to finish a deck. Unreviewed suggestions can remain until you return, but suggestions older than two weeks and cards based on an earlier month's figures are retired. Retirement is not treated as a dislike. Posts you deliberately kept or saved remain your work.

## How your corrections teach Market Trak

Edits in **Swipes, Content Packs, Property Packs and the calendar** feed the same learning system. Library and approval-queue edits contribute too. Paragraph breaks are preserved. Repeated corrections on different posts provide stronger evidence than repeatedly changing one post.

A corrected price, spelling or link does not become a general writing preference. Keeping posts about one property does not imply you want its features mentioned everywhere. Saving a post without editing it is not an endorsement of its style.

After every few passes, you can explain what was wrong. **Not needed right now** removes the decision from taste evidence. An explanation enriches the original pass rather than counting as another vote.

## Control what it learns

Open **Assistant memory > Content preferences** to:

- See up to **12 active preferences**, with supporting examples where available.
- Correct a preference. Your correction becomes confirmed and is not replaced automatically.
- Remove a preference, then restore it deliberately if you change your mind.
- Pause learning and application of inferred preferences. Confirmed corrections and explicit workspace instructions remain in force.

Older preferences are not removed simply because they are old. We merge similar ideas and favour stronger evidence. A removed category is not inferred again unless you restore it or provide an explicit instruction.

### Permanently delete a removed preference

Under **Removed preferences**, choose **Delete permanently** beside Restore, then confirm. The preference disappears from the list and cannot be restored. We retain a hidden block against learning it again; the deleted preference text and attached examples are removed. Your original post feedback remains separate.
