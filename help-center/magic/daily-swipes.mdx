**Swipes** is a short stack of finished posts waiting for you each day. Every card is written from your own area's market figures or one of your listings, with the image already made and your branding on it. You swipe right to keep a post, left to pass on it, and that is the whole job. A few seconds, once a day.

The point of the passing is not tidiness. Every keep and every pass teaches Market Trak what you like, and what it learns is used everywhere it writes for you, not just here.

Find it as **Swipes** at the top of the sidebar's Create group. On a phone, tap **Content** in the bottom bar and choose **Swipes**. Your dashboard also shows the next card waiting.

## What you get

- **A new set every day.** How many depends on your plan, and higher plans get more. If you start a free trial, your first set is made for you the morning after, so it is waiting when you come back; otherwise the first set is made the moment you open the deck, which takes about a minute. Once you have swiped before, the day's set starts writing as soon as you open Market Trak, so it is usually ready by the time you get to it. They are made once a day.
- **Cards you have not swiped stay put.** Come back later and they are still there. Nothing expires overnight and nothing is remade behind your back.
- **A mix of subjects.** Some cards are about a property you have listed, some are a single market figure in large type, and some are a wider post about the area. That mix is deliberate, so your feed does not read the same every week.
- **No credits.** The posts in your daily number are included in your plan, images and all.

<Callout kind="info">
Swipes are written from real figures for the postcodes you cover, so you need at least one report on your patch before the first deck can be made. If you see **Make your first report**, generate a report for your area and your swipes start from then on.
</Callout>

## Swiping

On a phone, drag the card right to keep it or left to pass. On any screen you can use the buttons instead.

The first time you open the deck, a short tour walks you through it: the card shows you the swipe, then each button and the history are pointed out in turn. It only runs once. If you closed it too soon, tap **How the deck works** under the card to see it again.

<Steps>
<Step title="Check where and when it is going" icon="calendar" titleType="p">
The bar above the card shows the channel and the date and time the post would go out. Tap it to change either. The time suggested is a real free slot on your calendar, picked around whatever you already have scheduled, so your posts never land on top of each other.
</Step>
<Step title="Read the post" icon="file-text" titleType="p">
The card shows the first few lines. Tap **Read the whole post** to see all of it, and **Edit** if you want to change the words before you keep it.
</Step>
<Step title="Keep, save or pass" icon="check" titleType="p">
**Keep** (the pink tick) puts the post on your calendar at the time on the bar. **Save** (the bookmark) puts it in your Library with no date, to use whenever you like. **Pass** (the cross) takes it out of the deck.
</Step>
</Steps>

**Where did this come from?** Under the buttons, this tells you which area and which month's figures the card was written from, or says plainly that there are no figures behind it and it is an opinion rather than a statistic.

<Callout kind="tip">
No Facebook Page connected yet? Keep still works, it just saves the post to your Library instead of your calendar, and tells you so.
</Callout>

## Changing your mind

Everything you have swiped today is listed under the deck, with what happened to it: **Scheduled** with its date, **Saved**, or **Passed**. Tap any of them to look again, and **Put it back in the deck** returns it so you can swipe it a second time. Putting back a scheduled post takes it off your calendar; anything you had saved stays in your Library.

When you have swiped the lot, the deck says so and there are no more until tomorrow. That is the idea: a small daily habit rather than a pile of work.

## What it learns from you

This is the part that works quietly in the background.

Every card you keep and every card you pass is a piece of evidence about your taste. Every third pass, Market Trak asks one quick question: was it the image, the writing, the subject, or the wrong area? One tap, and you can add a line of your own if you want to.

Once the same thing has come up a few times, it becomes a rule, something like "always mention the postcode district by name" or "avoid posts about interest rates". Those rules are then handed to everything that writes for you: your Content Packs, your property posts, the AI Assistant and tomorrow's swipes. So the deck is not only giving you posts, it is teaching the rest of Market Trak how you like to sound.

Answering the "why did you pass?" question counts for more than a silent pass, so it is worth the tap when you have an opinion.

<Callout kind="info">
Anything you have told the assistant directly, and any standing instructions you have set, always come first. What the swipes learn fills in behind them, it never overrules them.
</Callout>

## Questions

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Do I have to swipe them all?" default-open="false">
No. Cards you have not swiped are waiting whenever you next open the deck. There is no penalty for leaving them.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I get more than my daily number?" default-open="false">
Not today. One set a day, and when you have swiped through it that is the day's lot. Higher plans get a bigger set.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Do swipes use my credits?" default-open="false">
No. The posts in your daily number are included in your plan, including the images.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="I kept a post by mistake" default-open="false">
Find it in the list under the deck and choose **Put it back in the deck**. That cancels its calendar entry and you can swipe it again.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Why is a post about the postcode and not my town?" default-open="false">
Because that is what the figures actually cover. A district like CM15 is not the same area as the town it sits in, so a post that named the town would be quoting a number for somewhere else. You are welcome to edit the words before you keep a post.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Which plans include Swipes?" default-open="false">
Every paid plan includes them, and the number you get each day goes up with the plan. Free accounts do not have the deck. You can see what your plan includes on **Workspace settings > Billing & Subscription**.
</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
