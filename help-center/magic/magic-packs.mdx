The **Magic** page is the fastest way to produce a complete piece of marketing content in Market Trak. You type in one postcode, click **Generate**, and around ninety seconds later you have a finished local market report (with your logo and brand colour applied) and three matching social posts ready to publish or schedule.

Find it as the top item in the sidebar, marked with a sparkle icon.

## What's in a pack

Every Magic pack is one postcode in, four pieces of content out:

- **One local market report**, the same report you'd get from the Reports page. Average prices, demand rating, days on market, sold prices, and price-per-square-foot data for the postcode you typed. Your workspace logo and brand colour are applied automatically.

- **Three social posts**, each in a different visual style. You can post one a day across the week, or pick the one that fits the platform you're focused on. Every post:

  - Includes a working link back to your new report (in two of the posts) and a working link to your instant valuation page (in the third)
  - Has caption text written for the postcode and the live data in the report

The report is saved to your **Reports** page. The three posts are saved to your **Drafts** in the Social Media section so you can edit, schedule, or publish them whenever you like.

## How to generate a pack

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the Magic page" icon="sparkles" titleType="p">
Click **Magic** at the top of the sidebar.
</Step>
<Step title="Type a postcode" icon="map-pin" titleType="p">
Use just the first part of the postcode, e.g. `CM15` or `NW6`. Autocomplete suggests UK postcodes as you type.

If you paste a full postcode like `CM15 0QN` we automatically use only the first part for you. The reason: a full postcode narrows down to a single street, which thins out the market data. The outward code (the first part) covers a whole area and gives a much richer report.
</Step>
<Step title="Click Generate" icon="zap" titleType="p">
A single click kicks off the whole pack. You'll see a progress bar showing each stage: fetching market data, drafting the report, generating images, and writing the three captions.
</Step>
<Step title="Review your pack" icon="eye" titleType="p">
After around ninety seconds the report and three post mockups appear together on one page. From here you can:

- Click the report card to open the full report in a new tab
- Edit any caption inline by clicking the text
- Swap or edit the image on any post
- Save a post to drafts, schedule it, or publish straight to Facebook or Instagram
- Click **Generate another pack** to start a new pack for a different postcode
</Step>
</Steps>

## How the links inside posts work

Two of the three posts include a "Read the full report" link, and the third post includes a link to your instant valuation page. These links are filled in automatically after generation:

- The report link points to the **public version** of the report we just created for you. Anyone you share the post with can click through and read the report without logging in.

- The valuation link uses your **vanity URL** if you've set one (e.g. `marketrak.pro/valuation/your-agency`) and falls back to the standard valuation URL for your workspace otherwise.

You don't need to paste any URLs yourself. If you edit a caption, the link survives the edit.

## Tips for great packs

- **Pick areas you actually work in.** Magic packs work best in postcodes where your agency operates and the market data is rich. CM15, NW6, RG6, M1, anything that maps to a recognisable neighbourhood.

- **Generate one pack a week per area.** A pack a week keeps your social calendar full and your reports fresh. Once a postcode's market data has moved, regenerating gives you new numbers to talk about.

- **Edit the caption to match your voice.** Our AI gets you 95% of the way there but small tweaks make a post feel like you. The pencil icon on each post enters edit mode so you can change the caption, swap the image, or use the AI image-edit brush.

- **Save as draft if you're not ready to publish.** Every post on the Magic results page has a Save button. Saved posts go to your Drafts so you can come back to them later.

## Credits

Generating a Magic pack uses credits from your monthly allowance: one report's worth of credits plus three posts' worth, billed together. See [How Credits Work](/help-center/credits/how-credits-work) for the current cost per surface.

If you're on a workspace with monthly limits rather than credits, generating a pack counts as one report and three posts against your monthly limits.

## After generating

- The **report** lives on your [Reports page](/help-center/reports/generating-a-report). You can edit, share, regenerate, or track engagement on it like any other report.

- The **three posts** live in your [Drafts](/help-center/social-media/scheduling-and-publishing). Edit, schedule, or publish them whenever you like. They don't expire.

- Generate as many packs back-to-back as you need. There's no limit beyond your credits or monthly allowance.
