Once a month, Market Trak builds you a patch report: one page that shows where you stand across your postcode areas, built from live data at the moment you ask for it.

## Where to find it

Go to **Analytics** in the sidebar (under **Measure**) and open the **Your patch** tab. On your phone, open **Analytics** and tap **Your patch report** under **Go deeper**.

You will see one row for each month. The current month carries a **Generate** button until you build it; past months open instantly.

## Building this month's report

<Steps>
<Step title="Press Generate" icon="sparkles" titleType="p">
The report builds from live data in a couple of minutes. You can watch each section land, and it keeps building if you navigate away.
</Step>
<Step title="Read it" icon="book-open" titleType="p">
It opens with what stands out this month, then the numbers behind it.
</Step>
<Step title="Download it if you want it on paper" icon="download" titleType="p">
**Download PDF** at the top saves the report as a PDF to print or share with a colleague.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
A report is a snapshot of live data, so a month you never build is gone rather than waiting. Each new month unlocks on the 1st.
</Callout>

## What the report shows

- **What stands out** - the single most striking thing in this month's data, with the figures behind it.
- **You in Market Trak** - the same four tiles every month: posts published, email campaigns sent, contacts captured and packs generated, all over the last 30 days. From your second report, each tile shows the change against your previous report.
- **Your patch in numbers** - for each of your postcode areas: sold and asking averages, the year-on-year change in price per square foot, homes for sale, sales a month, months of stock and the market type.
- **Where the sales are, and where your listings are** - sales registered with HM Land Registry over the last 12 months, broken down by postcode sector with the busiest streets, next to how many of your own live listings sit in each. A listing whose address names only the town appears in an "Elsewhere in" row, so the totals always add up.
- **Who you are up against** - the agents Google lists for each of your towns, with their rating and review count. Click any name to open their Google listing and read their reviews.
- **Where to focus this month** - four actions picked from your own numbers, each one opening the tool that does it.

<Callout kind="tip">
"Sales registered" counts a sale when HM Land Registry records it, which is usually one to three months after completion. It is the official record, just a little behind the front door.
</Callout>

## Who gets it

Every paid plan includes a monthly patch report. A free trial includes one report. Your past reports are always kept, and you can open any of them from the list.

<Callout kind="info">
The report covers your postcode areas, so it needs at least one: an area joins your patch the first time you run a market report for it.
</Callout>
