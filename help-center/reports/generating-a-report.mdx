Property market reports are at the heart of Market Trak. Each report combines real market data (average prices, demand ratings, sold prices, days on market, and more) with a professional layout that you can share with potential sellers and landlords.

## How reports are created

There are two ways to create a report:

**As part of a Content Pack.** Type a postcode area, pick a report style, and the pack builds your report along with six matching social posts. This is the everyday route.

1. Click **Content Pack** in the sidebar

2. Type the postcode you want to cover and choose a report style

3. Click **Generate your pack**

**On its own, with the Report generator.** Click **Report** in the sidebar's Create section. Content Packs cover a postcode district (like M21) or, on paid plans, a sector (like M21 9), so use this when you need a report for a single full postcode or a town name, or when you want a report without the posts.

Either way, your report is ready in a couple of minutes and is saved to your **Library** (the **Reports** tab), where you can open it, copy its public link, rename it, and see its views and captured leads at a glance.

<Callout kind="tip">
You can also ask the [AI Assistant](/help-center/ai-assistant/overview) to create a report for a location.
</Callout>

## What data is included

Each report automatically includes:

- **Average property prices** for the area

- **Price change trends** (rising, falling, or stable)

- **Demand rating** (high, medium, or low)

- **Average days on market**

- **Sales volume** (transactions per month)

- **Sold prices** for comparable properties

- **Price per square foot** data

All data is sourced from the PropertyData API, which aggregates from Land Registry, EPC, and listing data.

### How the year-on-year figure is worked out

The price change on your report measures the **price per square foot** across recent sales in the area, measured over a full year and set against the year before. Measuring per square foot means the figure isn't swayed by whether larger or smaller homes happened to come up for sale in a given month, so it reflects the market rather than the mix of what was on it.

On the report the figure simply reads "year on year". If a client asks how it's worked out, this page is the answer.

Two things you may occasionally see:

- **"Not enough data"** in place of a percentage. Some areas have too few recent sales to give a reliable read, and we would rather say so than show you a flat result that isn't really flat.

- **"Based on the wider [postcode] area"** underneath the figure. If you asked for a small area such as a single postcode sector, we widen the search to its postcode district to find enough recent sales. The report tells you when it has done that, so the number you quote is always the number you can stand behind.

<Callout kind="info">
The average price shown on your report and this percentage measure different things. The average price is what homes in the area are currently being **marketed** at, taken from live listings. The percentage tracks what homes have actually **sold** for, per square foot. They will not match, and neither is a percentage of the other.
</Callout>

## Your reports stay up to date

When a [Content Pack](/help-center/magic/magic-packs) covers a postcode you've covered before, in the same report style, it refreshes your existing report in place instead of creating another copy:

- **One report per area, per style.** Your Library keeps a single up-to-date report for each postcode and style, rather than a growing stack of monthly duplicates.

- **The share link stays the same.** Because each new pack refreshes the report rather than replacing it, anything that links to it (posts, emails, your bio link) shows the latest figures after every new pack you generate for that area. Reports from before this change, and areas you stop covering, keep whatever data they had last.

- **Your edits are kept.** Custom call-to-action wording, email signup copy, and any sections you've hidden all survive the refresh; only the market data and commentary update.

## After generating

Once your report is ready, you can:

- Find it any time in your **Library** under the **Reports** tab

- [Edit and customise the content](/help-center/reports/editing-and-sharing)

- Share it with a public link

- Use it as the basis for email campaigns and social media posts

- Track who views it via [Engagement Tracking](/help-center/reports/engagement-tracking)

## Tips

- Reports are most valuable when they cover a specific area your agency operates in

- Regenerating a report refreshes the market data while keeping your custom edits

- Deleting a report from your Library stops its public link working, but any leads it captured stay safely in your Leads list
