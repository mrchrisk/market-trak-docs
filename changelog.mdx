Stay up to date with the latest Market Trak features, improvements, and fixes.

## August 2026

- **Your approval queue is now a tab, not a link you had to spot** - Posts waiting for approval used to sit behind a small link near the top of your content calendar. There is now an **Awaiting approval** tab next to Calendar, showing how many posts are waiting, so you can see at a glance without going looking. The morning digest email takes you straight to it, and any older link you have still works. [Read the guide](/help-center/social-media/approval-queue).

- **No more picking a writing style in the assistant** - The assistant used to stop and ask whether you wanted your post Conversational, Insight-led or Short & Punchy before it would write anything. That question has gone. Every post now comes out at one sensible length, the same length your Content Packs and property posts use, and if you want it different you just say so: "make that shorter", "give me a bit more detail". One less decision before you see a post. The caption style dropdown in the calendar composer is unchanged, because there you are genuinely choosing between three captions side by side. [Read the guide](/help-center/ai-assistant/creating-content).

- **Statement images now cost you nothing, and use your exact brand colour** - When you ask the assistant for a post in the Statement look, the picture is now drawn directly rather than generated. Your brand colour comes out exactly as you saved it rather than approximated, your own logo is placed on it, and the layout is consistent every time. Because nothing is generated, Statement images no longer use any of your credits. The other two looks, Boutique and Press, are unchanged. [Read the guide](/help-center/ai-assistant/creating-content).

- **Approving a post from your queue now checks your calendar first** - Your Content Packs and Property Packs already did this: if something has been scheduled close to a post's time since it was written, approving shifts it to the next clear slot so two posts never go out on top of each other. The approval queue does the same now, and the dialog tells you about any change before you confirm. A time you picked yourself is never moved. [Read the guide](/help-center/social-media/approval-queue).

- **Fixed: two properties found on the same day no longer get the same posting time** - When your feed picked up more than one listing in a day, every one of them was given the same three dates and times, so your queue showed several different properties all set to go out at the same moment. Suggested times are now picked around the posts already waiting in your queue, so each property gets its own slot: never two at once, never more than six in a day, and always at least an hour and a half apart. If a day fills up, the next post moves to the next day with room. These are the same good posting times your Content Packs and Property Packs use: 10am, 1pm and 4pm on a weekday, 11am, 2pm and 5pm at the weekend. Posts already waiting in your queue have been given tidy times too, so you will find them spread through the day rather than bunched up; each one kept its original day wherever there was room. Posts whose date has already passed are left alone, as before: they get a fresh date when you approve them, and approving a batch of them now spreads them properly. Several properties can share a day, but the same property never posts twice in one day, so a big catch-up takes a few days rather than a month. And if you have set a preferred posting time on your feed, it is now used properly: the hour was being ignored, so a feed set to 2:30pm was getting 9:30am. [Read the guide](/help-center/social-media/property-feed).

- **Your feed's three posts for a property no longer all end with the same link** - The three posts written for each new listing go out across five days. Previously all three finished with your property link, which reads repetitively and gets less reach, because Facebook and Instagram show link posts to fewer people. Now the first two invite people to message you, comment, tag a friend or save the post, each one different, and the third carries the link for anyone ready to see the full listing. They also use the same style of closing line as your Property Packs. If a feed is set to post automatically without your approval, it sticks to "message us" style closes, so nothing promises to reply to comments while you are not watching. [Read the guide](/help-center/social-media/property-feed).

- **The approval queue now opens on your newest property** - It used to open on your oldest scheduled post, so if you had a few waiting, today's arrivals were at the bottom. It now groups by property with the most recent first, and the tidier layout works properly on a phone: the buttons sit side by side instead of stacked, and Approve and Remove are where your thumb expects them. [Read the guide](/help-center/social-media/property-feed).

- **A morning round-up when your feed has posts waiting for approval** - When your property feed picks up new listings and writes posts for them, you now get one email the following morning showing the photos of what arrived, with a link straight to your approval queue. One email a day at most, and only on days something actually arrived, and it names which workspace it is about if you run more than one. It follows the **Posts ready for approval** switch in your notification settings, so turning that off turns this off too. [Read the guide](/help-center/social-media/property-feed).

- **Write notes on a lead after you have spoken to them** - Open any lead and there is now an **Add a note** box at the top of their activity timeline. Type what was said on the call and save it, and it sits in the timeline alongside everything else in date order, so you can see that you emailed someone on the Tuesday, that they opened it on the Wednesday, and that when you rang on the Thursday they said they were waiting until spring. Notes are shared with everyone in your workspace and show who wrote them, so a colleague picking up the same lead knows where things stand; you can edit or delete your own at any time. Once a lead has a note, a **Notes only** toggle appears above the timeline for when there is a lot of automatic activity to scroll past, and your main leads list shows a small note count against anyone you have written about, so you can see at a glance who has actually been spoken to. [Read the guide](/help-center/leads/managing-leads).

- **Fixed: images could disappear from a Content Pack left waiting** - Your posts keep their images for the full 30 days a pack is around. A background job that clears out pictures nothing is using any more had stopped seeing posts that were still very much in use, and was removing their images after a fortnight, leaving the words behind. That is fixed, and it now stops itself and raises a flag rather than ever clearing images in bulk again. Packs that had lost images have been removed rather than left looking half finished, so if one has gone from your account that is why, and a fresh pack takes about a minute from your dashboard. Anything you had already approved, scheduled or published is untouched, and so is every report in your Library. [Read the guide](/help-center/magic/magic-packs).

- **Signing up now picks up your phone number for you** - When you paste your website at the start, we read your agency phone number off it and show it back to you to check, alongside your logo and colours. Underneath, you can add a WhatsApp number, which switches on the WhatsApp button on your Bio link page so people can message you in one tap. Both are optional and neither holds up getting your first Content Pack, so skip them if you'd rather. You can add or change either one later under **Workspace Settings > Branding**. [Read the guide](/help-center/getting-started/welcome-flow).

- **Your dashboard now suggests setting up your Bio Link** - When everything more pressing is done, the **Today's Focus** card has one more suggestion: switch on your Bio Link. You'll only see it if your plan includes the Bio Link, you haven't set it up yet, and your Facebook page has an Instagram account linked. It disappears for good the day you switch the Bio Link on, and it never affects your Awareness score. [Read the guide](/help-center/leads/bio-link).

- **The setup guide no longer asks for tracking pixels** - Most agents don't have a pixel to hand, so that one optional step kept many setup guides from ever reaching 100%. It has been taken off the checklist. Tracking pixels themselves haven't moved: you can add them any time under **Settings > Integrations > Tracking**. And if pixels were the only thing between you and 100%, you'll find your guide complete next time you look. [Read the guide](/help-center/getting-started/setup-guide).

- **Fixed: property feeds now connect on many more agency websites** - Connecting your feed could come back with nothing found, even when you pasted the address of your own listings page and your site was being read perfectly well. Two separate causes, both now fixed: websites that keep properties under an address containing `/listings/`, and websites that write their links in a shorter form that we were not following. Feeds on those sites now connect on their own, from your web address alone. If you already got yours working by adding properties by hand, it is worth removing that and connecting your website properly, so new listings appear on their own from now on. [Read the guide](/help-center/social-media/property-feed).

- **Your year-on-year price figure is now a truer read on your market** - Every report and content pack carries a figure showing how prices in your area have moved over the past year. It is now measured per square foot, so it isn't swayed by whether larger or smaller homes happened to come up for sale in a given month. A quarter with a few more big detached houses no longer nudges it upward on its own. Because it is measured differently, a report you run today may show a different figure from one you ran last month for the same area, and in some places it will point the other way. Every report now says on the figure itself what it measures, so you and your client are looking at the same thing. Where an area is too small to give a reliable read, the report says so plainly instead of showing a flat result, and where we have looked at a slightly wider postcode to find enough recent sales, it tells you that too. [Read the guide](/help-center/reports/generating-a-report).

- **Fixed: some ad buttons stopped a lead ad from launching** - Choosing **Book Now** or **Contact Us** as the button on an ad that opens an enquiry form ended in a Facebook error at the final step, because Facebook only accepts certain buttons on that kind of ad. The Ad Launcher now only ever offers buttons Facebook will accept: the button choice has moved to sit with the **Where leads go** decision, so you choose what a click does first and then pick from the buttons that work for it, with **Apply Now** newly available. Ads that send people to your property page keep the full set, including Book Now and Contact Us. [Read the guide](/help-center/advertising/property-ad-launcher).

- **Schedule a campaign to send later** - Write a campaign now and set it to go out whenever suits, on a Tuesday morning or the first of the month. Choose **Schedule for later** in the send window and pick a date and time. When you have targeted by area, you also choose who it should reach when the time comes: everyone in those areas at that point, including leads who arrive between now and then, or only the leads on the list today. The first is the default, because more leads arriving is usually the reason to schedule ahead in the first place. Anything scheduled waits on the new **Scheduled** tab on your Leads page, showing when it goes out and how many people it reaches, and you can cancel it right up until it sends. Anyone who unsubscribes in the meantime is taken off automatically. [Read the guide](/help-center/leads/email-campaigns).

- **See how your Bio Link is performing** - A new **Bio link** tab in Analytics shows how many people open your Bio Link, which buttons they tap (your valuation, reports, website, Call, WhatsApp and your featured property), and, for the Property of the Week, how many opened it against how many registered their interest. The counts are anonymous. [Read the guide](/help-center/leads/bio-link).

- **Add Call and WhatsApp buttons to your Bio Link** - Put your phone number on your Bio Link so visitors can ring you or message you on WhatsApp in one tap, on a phone or a computer. Add your business phone and your WhatsApp number under **Settings > Branding**, then switch on the **Call** and **WhatsApp** buttons under **Leads > Bio link**. Your numbers are formatted automatically. Add the WhatsApp number even if it is the same as your business phone: we never assume the two match, since plenty of office numbers are landlines with no WhatsApp behind them. [Read the guide](/help-center/leads/bio-link).

- **Fixed: Instagram enquiries no longer read as Facebook on your Leads list** - When someone fills in one of your property ad forms on Instagram, we have always known it was Instagram, and the lead's record said so under **Where this lead came from**. Everywhere else still said Facebook: the Source column on your Leads list, the badge at the top of the lead, the line in their activity that read "Enquired via Facebook ad", and the Source column in an exported CSV. So the same lead could tell you two different things on one screen. All of those now name the network the form was actually filled in on. The filter above your list still groups both together, and now says so, because picking it returns your Facebook and Instagram enquiries alike. Nothing about your existing leads has changed, only what they are called. [Read the guide](/help-center/leads/lead-sources).

- **Fixed: the setup guide now responds straight away after you unlock** - When you unlocked everything from your Content Pack, the setup guide appeared on **Connect Facebook** looking perfectly normal, but for the first few seconds none of the buttons did anything: not Connect Facebook, not Skip, not the close button. Nothing had failed and nothing was still loading. The screen it opened over was still quietly swallowing every tap. The setup guide now takes your first tap the moment it appears. [Read the guide](/help-center/getting-started/free-trial).

- **Fixed: photos and suggestion lists in the assistant now work on a phone** - Three things inside the assistant were unresponsive on mobile, and only on mobile. Tapping a property photo opened it full screen but left you stuck there, with the close button, the arrows and the background all ignoring you. Typing a location or a postcode brought up a list of suggestions that could not be tapped, so you had to type the whole thing out yourself, and in the Content Pack flow that list was hidden behind the assistant altogether. All three now behave the same on a phone as they always have on a computer.

- **If your trial ends with posts still waiting, we now tell you while they can still go out** - Posts scheduled for after your trial ends stay on your calendar and wait for you to choose a plan. The problem was when we mentioned it: that email arrived up to nine days later, by which point the earliest of those posts had sat past their dates and needed a new date rather than simply going out. It now arrives three to five days after your trial ends, and it says what is actually waiting: how many posts, across how many properties, and when the next one is due to go. The help page also now explains the one rule that catches people out, which is that a post more than a day past its date waits on your calendar instead of publishing late, so a week's worth never goes out all at once the moment you subscribe. [Read the guide](/help-center/getting-started/free-trial).

- **Fixed: when someone unsubscribes using their mail app's own button, it now reaches your list** - Alongside the unsubscribe link inside your campaign emails, there is a hidden instruction that lets Gmail, Apple Mail and Yahoo show their own **Unsubscribe** button next to your name at the top of the message. That button was pointing at an address that could not act on it, so pressing it did nothing: the homeowner saw no error, assumed they were off your list, and carried on receiving your campaigns. The link inside the email always worked, so anyone who scrolled down and used that was removed properly. It is only the mail app's button that was failing, and it now takes them off your list the moment they press it. This matters for more than tidiness: Gmail and Yahoo expect that button to work and count it against senders whose does not, so a broken one was quietly working against your emails reaching the inbox at all. [Read the guide](/help-center/leads/email-campaigns).

- **You now get a confirmation email when you cancel** - Cancelling used to end on a screen and nothing else, so once you closed it there was nowhere to check the one thing you actually need later: the date your access runs to. You now get an email straight away that names that date, confirms you will not be charged again, and says what changes when the date passes: your reports go private, any links you have shared stop working, and scheduled posts stop publishing. Nothing is deleted, and everything you have made stays in your account. Alongside it, the **Billing Portal** window has stopped listing "downgrade or cancel" among the things it can do, because it cannot: changing your plan and cancelling both happen on the Billing page itself, and following that line would have sent you looking for a button that is not there. [Read the guide](/help-center/settings/billing).

## July 2026

- **Your dashboard now tells you to post what you've already written, and stops calling your back catalogue new** - Two changes to the **Today's Focus** card. If a pack has left finished posts sitting on its page, unscheduled, that is now the first thing the card asks you to deal with: it counts them and takes you straight to the pack, because nothing goes out until it is on your calendar and that takes a couple of minutes. It used to send you off to build another pack while the last one's posts sat there untouched. And when you first connect your property feed, everything already on your books arrives at once. The card was reading that as news and telling brand-new accounts things like "129 listings came in from your feed this week", then offering to build a Property Pack for one of them picked at random. Your opening import no longer counts as new arrivals, so the card only mentions a listing that genuinely came in after your feed was set up. [Read the guide](/help-center/dashboard).

- **You're now told when scheduled posts won't go out** - If your trial ends with posts still waiting on your calendar, those dated after it ends don't publish while you're on the free plan. That is on purpose: the report and valuation links inside them go private at the same moment, so publishing them would send homeowners to pages they can't see. What was missing was anyone telling you. Your reminder email the day before now names the number, for example "you have 25 posts scheduled for after tomorrow", and your dashboard carries the same reminder afterwards with a button through to your calendar. Nothing is deleted: choose a plan and they publish on the dates you set. [Read the guide](/help-center/getting-started/free-trial).

- **Your dashboard now tracks each of your areas separately** - One Content Pack used to mark your whole account as up to date, so if you cover five districts you could be told you were fresh on the strength of work in one of them. Your Awareness Meter now counts each area on its own and reads "3 of 5 areas covered", and the priority card names the area that has waited longest, for example "Your CM3 content is 41 days old", with a button that opens the pack builder with CM3 already filled in. An area counts as covered for a month, and you are only ever asked about one at a time, so it reads the same whether you cover three areas or ten. [Read the guide](/help-center/settings/postcode-areas).

- **Fixed: regenerating a report in a new area now asks you first** - If you regenerated a report for a district that is not one of your areas, you got a red "Regeneration Failed" message even when you had a spare area available, with no way to say yes. It now asks whether to add the area, exactly like generating a new report does, and gets on with the regeneration once you confirm. If your areas are all in use it tells you that instead of reporting a failure.

- **Your calendar now tells you when a post is not going to go out** - If your plan is inactive, whether that is a finished trial, a cancelled subscription or a payment that did not go through, your scheduled posts stay on your calendar but do not publish. That was true before and nothing on screen said so, which meant a calendar full of posts could look perfectly healthy while none of them were going anywhere. Those posts now show in amber and say plainly **On hold**, with the one line that matters: pick a plan and they publish on the dates you already set. Nothing is lost and nothing needs rewriting. You will also get one email at the point posting stops, telling you how many posts are waiting. Two other things changed alongside it. A post whose date passed more than a day ago now shows as **Missed** and will not publish by itself, so if your plan restarts after a break you will not suddenly fire out a fortnight of old posts in one go; pick a new date and it sends. And your calendar shows the same amber warning for those, so you can see at a glance what needs a new date. [Read the guide](/help-center/social-media/scheduling-and-publishing).

- **Fixed: posts scheduled on the hour now go out on time** - If you scheduled a post for a round time like 7:00, 10:00 or 1:00, it could arrive about fifteen minutes late, and once in a while it would sit on your calendar marked **failed** without having gone anywhere. Nothing was wrong with your Facebook or Instagram connection and no credits were charged. The cause was on our side: the hourly check that looks for new properties on your website ran at exactly the same moment as the posts, and on the hour is where most posts sit, because the times we suggest for your Content Pack are all round hours. With enough websites connected, that check crowded out the posting. It now runs at twenty past the hour instead, and it works through your properties steadily rather than all in one go. Posting itself is unchanged and still runs every fifteen minutes, so nothing you have already scheduled has moved. Separately, if a post does hit a hiccup on our side, it now quietly tries again on the next run instead of giving up, so a momentary glitch no longer costs you the post. If a post ever does end up failing for good, the message now tells you plainly that nothing was posted, rather than suggesting you go and check Facebook for something that was never sent. And a post you have rescued with **Publish Now** no longer keeps its old error message once it has gone out.

- **Your postcode areas** - Your plan covers a set number of postcode districts, and those are your areas. You will find them on the Billing page under **Your postcode areas**, with a count of how many you have left. An area is added the first time you make a report or a Content Pack for it, and you can add one yourself whenever you have room. Your areas now shape the rest of the app: the assistant offers them when it asks where something is for and pulls live market data for them, and campaigns suggest them first, with leads outside your areas marked **Out of area** and reachable through their own audience whenever you want them. If you want to work another district, extra areas are £25 a month each, added to your existing plan rather than a separate subscription, with the exact amount shown before anything is charged. [Read the guide](/help-center/settings/postcode-areas).

- **Fixed: your agency name now appears on your Property Pack posts** - The question-card photo in a Property Pack, the one with the cream card and a question across it, has a small line above the question meant to carry your agency name. It was coming out blank on every pack, for everyone. Your name now sits there as it was designed to, and the posts themselves are written knowing which agency they are for. Nothing to switch on: it uses your workspace name, so if that says what you want your customers to see, you are already set. If your workspace is still called "My Workspace", rename it and your name will start appearing. [Read the guide](/help-center/getting-started/workspace-setup).

- **Fixed: replies to your campaign emails now reach you** - When someone on your list replies to a market update, that reply comes to you and hitting reply puts you straight back in touch with them. Ordinary replies always arrived normally. What did not were the ones sent by mail systems that answer automatically: those go to the hidden address email providers use for delivery reports rather than to your reply address, so they never made it to you. They are now forwarded on automatically, addressed so that one tap of reply reaches the person who wrote. Empty automatic responses, like an out-of-office notice with nothing in it, are left out, so you only hear about a reply when there is actually something to read. [Read the guide](/help-center/leads/email-campaigns).

- **Your setup guide now ends with a one-minute video** - When you finish connecting your Facebook Page and your properties, the guide used to close on a written list of the weekly routine that gets the best out of Market Trak. It now plays you a short video of the same routine instead, showing the screens you'll actually use: run a Content Pack once a week, approve it, give a listing a Property Pack, and send your leads a market update each month. Press play when you're ready, or skip straight past it, the **Let's go** button works either way.

- **Videos now have a full screen button** - Any video in Market Trak, including the new setup video and the ones on our website, now has a full screen control in the bottom corner of the player, next to the time. Handy on a phone, or when you want to see the detail on screen.

- **Fixed: the headline on your weekly tip photo now always reads properly** - The short line printed on your Content Pack's seller-tip photo could occasionally come out as a garbled fragment rather than a sentence. Those lines are now written and checked separately before they go anywhere near the picture, so the panel always carries a clear, natural phrase, freshly worded for each pack, like "Ready to get sorted early?" on the get-your-paperwork-sorted tip.

- **Picking campaign areas now shows you when each one last heard from you** - When you choose which postcode districts to send a campaign to, each district now shows when its leads last received one, for example "emailed 2 days ago", or "never emailed" if nothing has ever gone to them. So while you are sending your usual area its update, you can see at a glance that the next district over has leads who have never heard from you, and including them is one tick. Automatic welcome emails do not count, only campaigns you actually sent. [Read the guide](/help-center/leads/email-campaigns).

- **The dashboard now tells you how many leads have never had an email from you** - Sending a campaign used to satisfy the "Leads to email" prompt even if it only went to part of your list, so leads who had never heard from you at all stayed invisible. When the prompt opens now, it tells you both things: how long since your last email, and how many of your leads have never received one, for example "89 of your 134 leads have never had an email from you". One campaign to your whole list catches everyone up.

- **Each lead's record now shows when you last contacted them** - Next to the date a lead was captured, their record now shows **Last contacted**, the date the most recent email went to them, or **Not emailed yet** if nothing ever has. Before this, a lead who never opened anything looked untouched, with no way to tell whether the silence was theirs or yours. [Read the guide](/help-center/leads/managing-leads).

- **The warm magazine image look is now called "Boutique"** - The look the assistant offers for your images, previously called Editorial, is now called **Boutique**, so it no longer shares a name with the Editorial Quote image templates. If you had already picked it, only the name changes. It also gained a third style of picture: the charming illustrated townscape from your weekly posts, with the little row of houses along the bottom. [Read the guide](/help-center/ai-assistant/creating-content).

- **Fixed: your dashboard no longer counts automatic welcome emails as campaigns** - The **Campaigns** figure on your dashboard was counting your automatic welcome email as a campaign you had sent, so an agent who had run one campaign saw "2". Every new lead quietly added another. Your figures have been corrected going back, so the number you see now reflects only campaigns you actually sent. The same correction applies to your weekly summary email and to your Awareness score, where a welcome email could make a brand new lead look like you had already followed them up. Your monthly email allowance is unchanged: welcome emails are real emails and still count towards it.

- **Fixed: the welcome email now reads properly when you don't know a new lead's area** - Your automatic welcome email is titled "Welcome to [your agency] - Your [area] Market Updates". When a lead came in without an area, the gap was filled with "your area", so the subject read "Your your area Market Updates". It now reads "Your local area Market Updates".

- **Fixed: emails now show their real subject line instead of `{{location}} Market Update - {{current_date}}`** - Wherever we showed you an email subject, on a lead's record and in the campaign open rates, you were seeing the raw template with the placeholders still in it. You now see the subject exactly as your recipients saw it, for example "KA7 Market Update - July". Your campaign open rates also used to be listed by template name ("Monthly Update - No link - 27/07/2026") rather than by the subject you actually wrote, which made it hard to tell one month's campaign from another. They are now listed by subject. This applies to your existing history too, not just new sends. [Read the guide](/help-center/leads/email-campaigns).

- **Automatic welcome emails no longer appear in your campaign open rates** - Your welcome email goes out to one new lead at a time, automatically. It was being listed in the campaign open-rate table as though it were a campaign you had sent, showing "1 recipient" and "100%", which flattered your overall figures and pushed the campaigns you actually ran further down the list. Welcome emails are now left out of that table and out of the dashboard's recent activity. They still appear on each individual lead's own record, because one really was sent to them. [Read the guide](/help-center/leads/email-campaigns).

- **A lead's record now shows the emails you sent them, not only the ones they opened** - Open any lead and their history used to start at the point they opened something. If you had emailed someone eight times and they had never once opened it, their record looked empty, as though nothing had ever happened, when in fact that is the lead you most want to know about. Every campaign email you have sent now appears in the timeline alongside their valuations, report opens and enquiries, and there is an **Emails sent** count next to **Email opens** so you can tell at a glance whether a quiet lead is ignoring you or has simply never been contacted. This is not just from today: your existing records fill in automatically, going back to October. [Read the guide](/help-center/leads/managing-leads).

- **Fixed: exporting your leads now gives you all of them, not just the page you were on** - Both **Export** buttons on the Leads page were only writing out the leads visible on screen. If you had 400 leads you got a file with 25 in it, and nothing told you the rest had been left out, so the file looked complete. Ticking **Select all** first made no difference. Exports now include every lead matching whatever filters you have set, whichever page you happen to be on, and the bulk **Export** includes leads you ticked on other pages. Three more things came out of the same fix: a lead whose name or address contains a comma no longer knocks the columns out of line in your spreadsheet (that was around one lead in seven), accented names open correctly in Excel, and leads whose email bounced now show as **Bounced** in the Status column rather than Active. [Read the guide](/help-center/leads/managing-leads).

- **Fixed: the Source filter on your Leads page now lists every source, not just the ones on screen** - The **Source** dropdown was only offering the sources it could find on the page you happened to be looking at. So if your first page was all Facebook ad leads, valuations were not in the list at all, and there was no way to filter to them short of paging through until one turned up. It now lists every source in your lead database, whichever page you are on. While fixing that we found the **Subscribed** option in the status filter was not doing anything either, so choosing it left you looking at everyone, including people who had unsubscribed or bounced. That works now too. [Read the guide](/help-center/leads/managing-leads).

- **Ask the assistant for a post about anything, not just about a report** - Every post you asked the assistant for used to be steered onto a market report. Ask for something else, like a post about your instant valuation tool or a few tips for selling in winter, and it would answer "Which area shall I build the post on?", and saying no just brought the question back. Now you get the post you actually asked for, written as your agency in your usual style and following the same rules as your Content Pack posts. Tips and advice posts arrive straight away, with no question at all.
